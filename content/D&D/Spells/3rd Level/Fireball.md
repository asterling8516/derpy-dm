# Fireball

**Fireball**
_3rd-level Evocation_
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Attack/Save:** None
**Range:** 150 ft
**Components:** V S M (a ball of bat guano and sulfur)
**Duration:** Instantaneous
**Damage/Effect:** 8d6 Fire damage

*<p>A bright streak flashes from you to a point you choose within range and then blossoms with a low roar into a fiery explosion. Each creature in a 20-foot-radius Sphere centered on that point makes a Dexterity save, taking 8d6 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

Flammable objects in the area that aren’t being worn or carried start burning.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 3.</p>*
