# Color Spray

**Color Spray**
_1st-level Illusion_
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Attack/Save:** None
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V S M (a pinch of colorful sand)
**Duration:** Instantaneous
**Damage/Effect:** Effect

*You launch a dazzling array of flashing, colorful light. Each creature in a 15-foot Cone originating from you must succeed on a Constitution save or have the Blinded condition until the end of your next turn.*
