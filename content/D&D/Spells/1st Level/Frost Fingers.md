# Frost Fingers

**Frost Fingers**
_1st-level Evocation_
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Attack/Save:** None
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V S
**Duration:** Instantaneous
**Damage/Effect:** 2d8 Cold damage

*<p>Freezing cold blasts from your fingertips in a 15-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a Constitution save, taking 2d8 cold damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

The cold freezes nonmagical liquids in the area that aren’t being worn or carried.

*****At Higher Levels.***** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 1st.</p>*
