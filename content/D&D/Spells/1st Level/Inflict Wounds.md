# Inflict Wounds

**Inflict Wounds**
_1st-level Necromancy_
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Attack/Save:** None
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V S
**Duration:** Instantaneous
**Damage/Effect:** 2d10 Necrotic damage

*<p>A creature you touch makes a Constitution save, taking 2d10 Necrotic damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d10 for each spell slot level above 1.</p>*
