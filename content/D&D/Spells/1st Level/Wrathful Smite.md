# Wrathful Smite

**Wrathful Smite**
_1st-level Evocation_
**Casting Time:** 1 bonus
**Attack/Save:** None
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V
**Duration:** 1 minute
**Damage/Effect:** 1d6 Psychic damage

*The next time you hit with a melee weapon attack during this spell’s duration, your attack deals an extra 1d6 psychic damage. Additionally, if the target is a creature, it must succeed on a Wisdom save or be frightened of you until the spell ends. As an action, the creature can make a [[/check wis format=long]] against your spell save DC to steel its resolve and end this spell.*
