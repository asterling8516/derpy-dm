# Cone of Cold

**Cone of Cold**
_5th-level Evocation_
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Attack/Save:** None
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V S M (a small crystal or glass cone)
**Duration:** Instantaneous
**Damage/Effect:** 8d8 Cold damage

*<p>You unleash a blast of cold air. Each creature in a 60-foot Cone originating from you makes a Constitution save, taking 8d8 Cold damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature killed by this spell becomes a frozen statue until it thaws.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 5.</p>*
