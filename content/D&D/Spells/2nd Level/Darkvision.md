# Darkvision

**Darkvision**
_2nd-level Transmutation_
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Attack/Save:** None
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V S M (a dried carrot)
**Duration:** 8 hour
**Damage/Effect:** Effect

*For the duration, a willing creature you touch has [[General D&D/Spells/2nd Level/Darkvision]] with a range of 150 feet.*
