# Enthrall

**Enthrall**
_2nd-level Enchantment_
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Attack/Save:** None
**Range:** 60 ft
**Components:** V S
**Duration:** 1 minute
**Damage/Effect:** Effect

*You weave a distracting string of words, causing creatures of your choice that you can see within range to make a Wisdom save. Any creature you or your companions are fighting automatically succeeds on this save. On a failed save, a target has a −10 penalty to Wisdom ([[Perception]]) checks and Passive [[Perception]] until the spell ends.*
