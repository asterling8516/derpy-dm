# Greater Invisibility

**Greater Invisibility**
_4th-level Illusion_
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Attack/Save:** None
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V S
**Duration:** 1 minute
**Damage/Effect:** Effect

*A creature you touch has the Invisible condition until the spell ends.*
