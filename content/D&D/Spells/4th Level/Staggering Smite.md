# Staggering Smite

**Staggering Smite**
_4th-level Evocation_
**Casting Time:** 1 bonus
**Attack/Save:** None
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V
**Duration:** 1 minute
**Damage/Effect:** 4d6 Psychic damage

*The next time you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack during this spell’s duration, your weapon pierces both body and mind, and the attack deals an extra 4d6 psychic damage to the target. The target must make a Wisdom save. On a failed save, it has disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks, and can’t take reactions, until the end of its next turn.*
