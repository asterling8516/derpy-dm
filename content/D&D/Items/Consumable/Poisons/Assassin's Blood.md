*<p>*Ingested Poison*

A creature subjected to Assassin’s Blood makes a DC 10 Constitution save. On a failed save, the creature takes  [[/damage 1d12 type=poison average=true]] damage and has the Poisoned condition for 24 hours. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage only.</p>*
