# Oil of Taggit (Contact)

**Oil of Taggit (Contact)**
_Potion_
**Type:** Potion
**Quantity:** 1
**Weight:** N/A
**Price:** 400 gp
**Rarity:** 
**Attunement:** None

*<p>A creature subjected to this poison must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution save or become poisoned for 24 hours. The poisoned creature is unconscious. The creature wakes up if it takes damage.

**Contact.** Contact poison can be smeared on an object and remains potent until it is touched or washed off. A creature that touches contact poison with exposed skin suffers its effects.</p>*
