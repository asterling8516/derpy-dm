# Oil of Taggit

**Oil of Taggit**
_Potion_
**Type:** Potion

**Rarity:**

*<p>*Contact Poison*

A creature subjected to Oil of Taggit must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution save or have the Poisoned condition for 24 hours. The creature also has the Unconscious condition while Poisoned in this way. It wakes up if it takes damage.</p>*
