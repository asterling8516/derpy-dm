# Holy Water

**Holy Water**
_Trinket_
**Type:** Trinket
**Quantity:** 1
**Weight:** 1 lb
**Price:** 25 gp
**Rarity:** 
**Attunement:** None

*When you take the Attack action, you can replace one of your attacks with throwing a flask of Holy Water. Target one creature you can see within 20 feet of yourself. The target must succeed on a Dexterity save or take  [[/damage 2d8 type=radiant average=true]] damage if it is a Fiend or an Undead.*
