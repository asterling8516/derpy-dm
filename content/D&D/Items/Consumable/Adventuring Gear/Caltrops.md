# Caltrops

**Caltrops**
_Trinket_
**Type:** Trinket
**Quantity:** 20
**Weight:** 0.1 lb
**Price:** 1 gp
**Rarity:** 
**Attunement:** None

*As a Utilize action, you can spread Caltrops from their bag to cover a 5-foot-square area within 5 feet of yourself. A creature that enters this area for the first time on a turn must succeed on a **DC 15 DEX** saving throw or take **1 Piercing** damage and have its Speed reduced to 0 until the start of its next turn. It takes 10 minutes to recover the Caltrops.*
