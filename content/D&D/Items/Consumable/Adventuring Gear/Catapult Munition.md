# Catapult Munition

**Catapult Munition**
_Trinket_
**Type:** Trinket
**Quantity:** 1
**Weight:** N/A
**Price:** N/A
**Rarity:** 
**Attunement:** None

A catapult munition roughly the size and weight of a cannonball.

*The munition can be thrown up to 30 feet and explodes on impact. Any creature within a 15-foot-radius sphere centered on the point of impact must make a **DC 14 DEX** saving, taking **10d6 Fire** damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. In addition, each object in that area that isn’t being worn or carried takes **1d4 Fire** damage.*
