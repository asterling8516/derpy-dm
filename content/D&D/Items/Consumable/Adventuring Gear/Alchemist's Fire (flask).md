# Alchemist's Fire (flask)

**Alchemist's Fire (flask)**
_Potion_
**Type:** Potion
**Quantity:** 1
**Weight:** 1 lb
**Price:** 50 gp
**Rarity:** 
**Attunement:** None

*This sticky, adhesive fluid ignites when exposed to air. As an action, you can throw this flask up to 20 feet, shattering it on impact. Make a ranged attack against a creature or object, treating the alchemist's fire as an improvised weapon. On a hit, the target takes **1d4 Fire** damage at the start of each of its turns. A creature can end this damage by using its action to make a **DC 10 DEX** Save to extinguish the flames.*
