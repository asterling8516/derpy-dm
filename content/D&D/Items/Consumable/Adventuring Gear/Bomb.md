# Bomb

**Bomb**
_Trinket_
**Type:** Trinket
**Quantity:** 1
**Weight:** 1 lb
**Price:** 150 gp
**Rarity:** 
**Attunement:** None

*As an action, a character can light this bomb and throw it at a point up to 60 feet away. Each creature within 5 feet of that point must succeed on a **DC 12 DEX** saving throw or take **3d6 fire** damage.*
