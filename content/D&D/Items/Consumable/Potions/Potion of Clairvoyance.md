# Potion of Clairvoyance

**Potion of Clairvoyance**
_Potion_
**Type:** Potion
**Quantity:** 1
**Weight:** N/A
**Price:** 2000 gp
**Rarity:** Rare
**Attunement:** None

*<p>When you drink this potion, you gain the effect of the Clairvoyance spell (no Concentration required).
