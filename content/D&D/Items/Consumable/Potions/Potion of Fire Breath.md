# Potion of Fire Breath

**Potion of Fire Breath**
_Potion_
**Type:** Potion
**Quantity:** 1
**Weight:** N/A
**Price:** 200 gp
**Rarity:** Uncommon
**Attunement:** None

*<p>After drinking this potion, you can take a Bonus Action to exhale fire at a target within 30 feet of yourself. The target makes a DC 13 Dexterity save, taking  [[/damage 4d6 type=fire average=true]] damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. The effect ends after you exhale the fire three times or when 1 hour has passed.

This potion’s orange liquid flickers, and smoke fills the top of the container and wafts out whenever it is opened.</p>*
