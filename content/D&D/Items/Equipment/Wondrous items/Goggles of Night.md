# Goggles of Night

**Goggles of Night**
_Trinket_
**Type:** Trinket
**Quantity:** 1
**Weight:** N/A
**Price:** 400 gp
**Rarity:** Uncommon
**Attunement:** None

*While wearing these dark lenses, you have [[General D&D/Spells/2nd Level/Darkvision]] out to 60 feet. If you already have [[General D&D/Spells/2nd Level/Darkvision]], wearing the goggles increases its range by 60 feet.*
