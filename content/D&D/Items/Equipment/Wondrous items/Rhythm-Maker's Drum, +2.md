# Rhythm-Maker's Drum, +2

**Rhythm-Maker's Drum, +2**
_Trinket_
**Type:** Trinket
**Quantity:** 1
**Weight:** N/A
**Price:** 13000 gp
**Rarity:** Rare
**Attunement:** Requires Attunement
