Mother is an ancient being who claims to have been alive long enough to watch dragons grow old and die. She once used her power to take life from living things to prolong her own, though after her daughters and single son left after witnessing her deeds she has vowed to change her ways though this has caused time to begin catching up with her. After having helped defend her and her daughter Ursula she offered to help them in return giving up most of her remaining time she was able to break the pact between some friendly passersby and Happily Ever After.
