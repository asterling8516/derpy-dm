The **Looking Glass Coven** is a widespread, deeply entrenched society dedicated to the pursuit, preservation, and dissemination of magical knowledge across Eureka. While many factions hoard power, the Coven believes that knowledge kept hidden stagnates, and magic should be understood and accessible (albeit safely). 

Because Eureka is a collision of four different planes (which formed the Four Kingdoms), the Coven has decentralized its operations. They have established four primary "sects," one in each Kingdom, specializing in the schools of magic that naturally resonate with the lingering planar energies of that region. 

## The Main Base: The Grand Reflection
While each sect has its own headquarters, the true heart of the Looking Glass Coven does not exist in any of the four Kingdoms. Their main base is **The Grand Reflection**—an expansive demiplane resembling an infinite, crystalline library. It can only be accessed by high-ranking members who step through specially enchanted mirrors hidden within each of the four kingdom bases. This ensures that even if a sect is raided or a kingdom falls, the Coven’s accumulated knowledge remains safe.

## Ranks within the Coven
- **Seeker:** The lowest rank. Field agents, adventurers, and scholars tasked with finding lost magic, artifacts, and historical texts.
- **Reflector:** Mid-tier members. Teachers, researchers, and scribes who study the magic brought in by Seekers and figure out how to safely share it. They often act as gatekeepers for the Prisms.
- **Prism:** The guild leaders of the four sects. Masters of their respective magical schools who oversee operations in their Kingdom.
- **The Silvered Eye:** The mysterious grandmaster of the Coven who resides permanently in The Grand Reflection. Their true identity is unknown, communicating only through reflective surfaces.

## The Four Sects

### 1. Kingdom of Diamonds (Grimhold) - The Feywild Sect
* **Specialty:** Illusion & Enchantment (Charm)
* **Base:** The Hall of Mirrors 
* **Prism:** **Elara "The Painted Lady" Vance.** A charismatic and terrifyingly beautiful Archfey illusionist who treats magic like high art. `᚛ᚋᚐᚊᚔ᚜ ᚛ᚈᚒᚐᚈᚔ᚜`
* **The Reflector:** **Vespera the Weaver.** A dryad `᚛ᚋᚐᚊᚔ᚜ ᚛ᚈᚒᚐᚈᚔ᚜`

### 2. Kingdom of Spades (Shadows Haven) - The Shadowfell Sect
* **Specialty:** Necromancy & Divination
* **Base:** The Obsidian Oubliette (An expansive, multi-level catacomb beneath a supposedly abandoned graveyard).
* **Prism:** **Morven Ashweaver.** A pale, softly-spoken Shadar-Kai. Shadows Haven is known as an outlaw city, making it the only place where the Coven can openly study Necromancy without facing the wrath of paladins or clerics. Morven’s sect focuses on "ethical necromancy"—speaking with dead historians to uncover lost spells, and using skeletal constructs purely for archiving and physical labor in dangerous ruins.
* **The Reflector:** **Grave-Speaker Yorick.** A gloomy but surprisingly chatty revenant scholar who manages the "Reading Room" in the upper levels of the catacombs. He bars access to the deep Oubliette. To earn an audience with Morven, outsiders must prove their worth to Yorick.

### 3. Kingdom of Clubs (Frostlight) - The Material Plane Sect
* **Specialty:** Abjuration & Evocation
* **Base:** The Bureau of Theoretical Abjuration
* **Prism:** **Silas Thorne.** A stern, heavily armored dwarf who appears more like a military general than a wizard.
* **The Reflector:** **Chief Inspector Vane.** `᚛ᚋᚐᚊᚔ᚜ ᚛ᚈᚒᚐᚈᚔ᚜`
* **The Iron Law Loophole:** `᚛ᚋᚐᚊᚔ᚜ ᚛ᚈᚒᚐᚈᚔ᚜`

### 4. Kingdom of Hearts (Infernium) - The Nine Hells Sect
* **Specialty:** Transmutation & Conjuration
* **Base:** The Shifting Sanctum
* **Prism (Leader):** **Archmage Malphas.** A highly sophisticated, sharply dressed Rakshasa (fiend) who views magic as the ultimate currency. Because the Kingdom of Hearts is heavily influenced by the Nine Hells, this sect focuses on the physical manipulation of reality and binding rituals. `᚛ᚋᚐᚊᚔ᚜ ᚛ᚈᚒᚐᚈᚔ᚜`
* **The Gatekeeper:** **Kallista (Reflector).** A tiefling alchemist bound by a magically enforced non-disclosure agreement. `᚛ᚋᚐᚊᚔ᚜ ᚛ᚈᚒᚐᚈᚔ᚜`
