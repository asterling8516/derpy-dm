The founder of the Looking Glass Coven is a figure known today only as **The Silvered Eye**, though historical records (the few that haven't been confiscated by the Silent Archives) suggest her true name was **Lysandra Glass**.

## The Ideological Foil
To understand the creation of the Looking Glass Coven, one must understand the Great Cataclysm. When the sorcerer known as `ⵜⵉⴼⵉⵏⴰⵖ ⴰⴱⵊⴰⴷ` The First Scribe accidentally shattered the planes, he was horrified by his own ambition. He concluded that knowledge was too dangerous for the world and founded the Silent Archives to hoard and hide it away, believing ignorance was the only path to safety.

Lysandra was a peer—and some say a fierce rival—of `ⵜⵉⴼⵉⵏⴰⵖ ⴰⴱⵊⴰⴷ`. She witnessed the exact same devastation as the planes collided to form Eureka, but she came to the exact *opposite* conclusion. She believed that the Cataclysm didn't happen because `ⵜⵉⴼⵉⵏⴰⵖ ⴰⴱⵊⴰⴷ` knew too much; it happened because he worked in isolated secrecy. If his theories on the magics of the strange book he had found were peer-reviewed, shared, and studied by a diverse group of mages, the disaster could have been prevented, or at least mitigated.

To Lysandra, hiding magic in the dark only ensures that the next time someone stumbles upon a dangerous spell, they won't know how to control it.

## The Shattering and the First Mirror
During the height of the Cataclysm, as raw planar energy tore through reality, Lysandra desperately sought a way to stabilize the localized gravity and magical weaves around her fleeing people. She discovered a fascinating property of magical mirrors: because they perfectly reflect reality, they could be used to anchor and stabilize small pockets of space.

She used this theory to craft a massive, crystalline sanctuary outside of normal space and time, saving thousands of scholars and magical texts from being annihilated by the planar collision. This sanctuary became **The Grand Reflection**.

## The Formation of the Coven
Safe within the Grand Reflection, Lysandra formed the Looking Glass Coven. She established the four planar sects to ensure that magical knowledge was decentralized—never again hoarded by a single person in a single place. The Coven's core tenet was born from her rivalry with The First Scribe: *Magic is a tool, not a terror. It must be studied, understood, and shared, so that the people of Eureka are never left defenseless against the unknown.*

## The Sacrifice
To maintain the immense magical anchor holding The Grand Reflection together, Lysandra made a permanent sacrifice. She bound her physical form and her soul into the crystalline architecture of the demiplane itself.

She ceased to be a mortal woman and became **The Silvered Eye**, the eternal Grandmaster of the Coven. She can never leave The Grand Reflection, and she communicates with her Prisms and Reflectors purely by appearing in mirrors and reflective surfaces across the Four Kingdoms. She watches, she guides, and she ensures the pursuit of knowledge never stops.
