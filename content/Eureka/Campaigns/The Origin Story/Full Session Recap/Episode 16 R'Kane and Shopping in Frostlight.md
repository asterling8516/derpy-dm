# Campaign Recap: The Ticking Spire of Frostlight

## Whispers in the Wind & Preparations

Following their narrow escape from the toxic perils of Mistvale and the sub-cathedral beneath it, the crew gathered aboard their magical airship. Though a lingering debate persisted over whether the vessel’s magical propulsion system was flying them forward or perpetually in reverse, the destination was set: **Frostlight**, the capital of the Kingdom of Spades.

Before departing, the party took time to rest, restock, and discuss their battle tactics. 
* **Kikush (Cuckooche)**, the brilliant artificer, sought to acquire rare components for his magic—specifically a gilded playing card worth 400 gold pieces to summon a dread spirit. 
* **Sylvia** prepared her defenses, stepping into her role as a heavily armored, frontline protector.
* **Slicer** and **Garp** aligned their deadly single-target capabilities, while **Finn** prepared to unleash devastating area-of-effect spells.

With pockets heavy from their recent exploits, the party purchased a stockpile of healing potions from the Happily Ever After reserves, enforcing a strict carrying limit of five potions per person, with the rest safely stowed away in their shared *Bag of Holding*.

---

## Crossing the Iron Meadow

As the airship soared above the clouds, the scenic beauty of giant, peaceful sky-mantas gliding through the slipstream was suddenly cut by a grim sight below. Peering over the railing, the party looked down upon **the Iron Meadow**.

```
    THE IRON MEADOW
    A scarred, barren wasteland between the four kingdoms where the catastrophic planar collision once took place. Piles of rusted swords and hollow armor litter the dead earth. It is a cursed dead zone; those who enter on foot rarely return, their spirits seemingly lost to the shifting metal.
```

Through the haze, the crew caught fleeting glimpses of what appeared to be empty armor moving on its own, and phantom figures picking through the debris—only to vanish when they blinked. Steering clear of the cursed ground, the ship forged ahead toward the glowing horizon.

---

## Frostlight, the Living Machine

As the airship approached its destination, the cold northern air gave way to a spectacular sight. Frostlight was a marvel of logic, magic, and clockwork technology.

* **The Gears of the Grove:** The city’s foundations rest upon colossal, magically infused trees. Their trunks are interwoven with polished brass pipes, shimmering copper wire veins, and massive clockwork gears.
* **Hardened Ice & Steel:** Spires of magically reinforced ice rise alongside towers of polished steel. The entire metropolis hums with clean energy drawn from mountain geysers.
* **The Iron Law:** Under the rule of King Theron Vane (the Chronometer) and Queen Briar Lockwell (the Weaver), magic is strictly regulated. It may only be used for *creation*. To "un-create" or destroy—even to dispel an enchantment or remove a curse—is considered a heinous crime against the natural order.

The airship docked at **The Spire**, Happily Ever After’s local headquarters. Massive iron clockwork dwarves and metallic hounds patrolled the clean, orderly streets below.

---

## Contracts and Compromises

Upon docking, Sylvia was presented with her newly forged, heavy Adamantite plate armor, delivered by a clockwork automaton. As she donned the steel, her mystical flower began to glow, emitting a sweet, slightly burnt fragrance, and pointed directly toward the royal castle in the distance. 

Inside the Spire, Sylvia was led to a sterile, disinfectant-scented laboratory to finalize her employment contract with Happily Ever After. 
* **The Negotiations:** Sylvia flatly refused to have the standard "mana gem" (a tracking and communication device) surgically implanted into her body. 
* **The Compromise:** Intrigued by her nature as a half-angel, the supervising automaton, Piper, offered a compromise. In exchange for undergoing a battery of magical scans and diagnostic tests, Sylvia was granted an external, handheld communication device. She signed a magical non-disclosure contract, swearing never to reveal the nature of the study.

---

## The Tinkered Tail

While Sylvia was being studied, the rest of the party wandered into a local workshop called *The Tinkered Tail*, run by an elven woman named Elloween and her dwarf husband, Bram. The shop was filled with mechanical beasts—owls with leather wings, brass spiders, and clockwork snakes.

```
    SHOPPING & UPGRADES
    • Kikush purchased a prototype clockwork owl (resembling his lost companion, Glimmer) for 300 GP.
    • Garp purchased a clockwork snake equipped with a two-minute video recording device for 325 GP.
    • Finn purchased an upgraded mechanical spider with cold-resistant rim-steel plating and a high-difficulty web trap (DC 18) for 775 GP, trading a valuable emerald eye to cover the cost.
```

To bypass the shop's massive year-and-a-half-long waiting list for these custom upgrades, Slicer employed a highly unconventional distraction. Utilizing his supernatural charisma, Slicer flirted shamelessly with both Elloween and Bram, successfully charm-talking his way into a private back-room "negotiation" that left the married couple thoroughly flustered and more than willing to prioritize the party’s requests.

---

## The Shattered Pantheon & Arcane Chaos

Seeking spiritual answers, the party visited a local cathedral—a breathtaking hollow spire of solid ice. Inside, however, they found a scene of desperation. 

The ice statues of the gods were literally cracking and collapsing. Clerics and wizards worked tirelessly, using magic to reconstruct them, only for the limbs of deities like Gond to shatter and fall to the floor moments later. The clerics whispered a grim truth: *the gods are dying, or already dead.*

* **A Tribute to the Forgotten:** Moved by the sight, Finn used his magical tattoos as a reference to shape a beautiful statue of **Lady Seraphina** out of solidified dust, carving a message at its base: *"If you feel lost or forgotten, just remember you're not the only one."*

### The Arcane Incident
In a quiet corner of the cathedral, the party encountered a group of elves wearing purple and yellow theater masks. They were worshipers of **The Fates**, and they were heavily indulging in **Arcane**—a highly volatile, lightning-infused powdered drug.

* Slicer, seeking a rush, snorted a line of Arcane off the flat of his blade. Instantly, his speed doubled, and crackling blue lightning enveloped his body.
* Finn, succumbing to chaotic impulse, snatched a vial of the drug and bolted out of the church.
* A chaotic chase ensued. Sylvia unfurled her wings, flying out of the cathedral to tackle Finn to the cobblestones. Slicer joined the wrestling match, wrestled the stolen vial away, and promptly consumed it himself.

Double-dosed on Arcane, Slicer became a hyper-accelerated, walking flashbang. His mind racing and his body surging with electricity, he took off sprinting wildly through the streets of Frostlight. To make matters worse, Slicer's talking patron blade began to whisper in his drug-addled mind: *his "brother"—the sister sword from the ancient ruins—was somewhere nearby.*

As Garp shook his head and walked off to the *Low Beam Tap Room* to get a stiff drink, Sylvia’s handheld device buzzed with a new directive from Happily Ever After:

> **MISSION DIRECTIVE:**
> Head to the Black Forest. Speak with "Mother" regarding her daughter, Ursula. Broker a negotiation to halt the relentless attacks of the Black Mantas. 
> *Disclaimer: Happily Ever After is "Only a Wish Away" and cannot be held liable for any side effects of said wish.*

The session ended with the party scattered, a new quest on the horizon, and Slicer running loose in the clockwork city, high on lightning.
