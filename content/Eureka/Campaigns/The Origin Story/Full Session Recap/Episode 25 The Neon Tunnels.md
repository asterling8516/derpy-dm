# D&D Session Recap: Neon Depths & Equine Afflictions

## The Omen in the Sky

The party found themselves camping a few miles outside the sandstone walls of **Oasis City**. Under the baking heat of the desert sun, they tended to their mounts—including Garp’s beloved giant lizard, "Sonata"—while waiting out a series of bizarre, debilitating afflictions. 

*   **S Licer** was utterly convinced he was a thoroughbred horse, trotting on all fours.
*   **Kookoush** was floating aimlessly, fully believing himself to be a frog.
*   **Silvia** was cursed to weep uncontrollably whenever she uttered a lie.

As they rested, the fabric of reality suddenly tore open. A sound like shattering glass and tearing metal echoed from above. For a terrifying moment, the bright daylight vanished, replaced by a yawning void. Within the darkness, a chaotic landmass of raging waters, erupting volcanoes, and crumbling mountains loomed close. 

Fynn immediately recognized this cosmic anomaly as the **Elemental Chaos**. Just as quickly as it had approached, the mass receded, shrinking back into the void as the sun reclaimed the sky. 

In the quiet that followed, a booming voice echoed in Fynn’s mind. It was Talos, delivering a cryptic command:
> *"Seek out the silent archives and the one known as Codex... a sword, a quill, and a book."*

***

## Infiltration & The Ziggurat Baths

With wanted posters bearing their faces plastered across the region, the party knew they couldn't enter Oasis City looking like themselves. S Licer, momentarily channeling his inner artisan despite his equine delusions, broke out a disguise kit. He meticulously equipped everyone with oversized fake mustaches and glasses. 

Thus "disguised," they entered the city. Oasis City was a breathtaking sight—a blend of ancient sandstone architecture and sci-fi grandeur, dominated by flashing corporate holograms from the *Happily Ever After* corporation. Amidst the neon advertisements, however, were hundreds of missing persons posters. 

Seeking a cure for their curses, the party made a beeline for the central **Ziggurat**, beneath which lay the fabled healing springs of "The Source." 

### The Equine Misunderstanding
At the bathhouse entrance, the dwarven attendants demanded a steep fee of 100 gold pieces per person. When Garp jokingly tried to pass off the cloaked, trotting S Licer as a "stray horse" to avoid paying for him, the pragmatic dwarves promptly dragged S Licer into a back alley to euthanize him. Realizing the joke had gone too far, Garp rushed to the alley and rescued the horse-brained S Licer just before the hammer fell.

### The Forged Groupon
Desperate to save coin, Garp ducked behind Silvia and forged a highly official-looking "Multi-Pass." With a winning smile and a silver tongue, he convinced the dwarves it was a corporate "Groupon," successfully bargaining the price down to a mere 50 gold pieces per head. 

The party submerged themselves in the glowing, cool waters of the spring. Within an hour, the magic of the waters washed away their afflictions:
*   S Licer stood on two legs again, his humanity restored.
*   Kookoush drifted back to the ground, no longer a frog.
*   Silvia’s tear ducts returned to normal function.

***

## The Neon Canopy & Clues of the Lost

Freshly cured and thoroughly washed, the party sought information regarding Garp’s missing family: his wife, **Coralai** (a red-headed, freckled human with emerald eyes), and his young son, **Caspian**. 

They headed to **The Neon Canopy**, a bustling, rebellious tavern coated in anti-corporate graffiti. Behind the bar stood a bipedal, crystalline ant-person who spoke Common via a glowing translation crystal held in his mandibles. 

The party ordered rounds of **Shadow Stout**—a murky import from the Shadowfell that tasted like garbage but granted the drinkers temporary dark vision. 

When questioned about Coralai, the bartender’s crystal clicked as he recalled her:
*   She had been seen a few weeks prior, traveling with a group of nomadic Eladrin on a massive "sand-boat" (an airship designed to traverse the dunes).
*   They were last seen heading toward the mountains between Oasis City and Grimhold.
*   However, she hadn't returned for supplies in weeks—highly unusual for the nomads.
*   The bartender also noted that people had been vanishing off the streets. A "crazy dwarf" in town insisted that something sinister was hunting from beneath the Ziggurat.

While the party conversed, Kookoush studied the bartender's translation crystal. Utilizing his arcane knowledge and recalling the crystal spiders they had fought in a previous dungeon, Kookoush realized he could replicate these translation devices using the materials currently in his possession. He also learned the creator of the crystal was a mysterious figure named **Suncatcher**, who resided deep in the desert.

***

## Descent into the Neon Depths

As the party stepped out of the tavern, Silvia’s magical flower suddenly began to reek of rotting flesh—a dark omen. A split-second later, a brief, muffled scream echoed from a nearby alleyway, followed by dead silence.

Investigating the alley, they found a five-foot-wide sinkhole where the sandstone street had crumbled into swirling black sand and shards of obsidian. 

Silvia volunteered as bait. Tied to a 50-foot rope, she stepped into the quicksand and began to sink. The rope went entirely taut, but she found no bottom—only a vast, empty drop. 

Deciding that fortune favors the bold, the party resolved to dive in. Kookoush threw his mechanical spider drone down first, and one by one, the adventurers jumped into the devouring sand.

### The Underground Cavern
They plummeted through the darkness, landing safely in a shallow, underground lake. The cavern was massive, damp, and bizarrely illuminated by a kaleidoscope of glowing mushrooms, crystals, and hijacked *Happily Ever After* neon signs. 

Near their landing zone, they found discarded shoes and a scattered trail of mismatched clothing. Sifting through the water, Silvia retrieved a glistening locket. Inside was a portrait of a red-headed woman and a sandy-haired boy—**Coralai and Caspian**. Garp’s family had definitely been dragged down here.

Further investigation revealed a ransacked, overgrown campsite. Kookoush and Silvia identified goblin footprints in the sand, though the tracks exhibited a strange, magical property—disappearing and abruptly reappearing twenty feet further down the tunnel, as if the creatures were short-range teleporting.

***

## The Obsidian Rave

Following the teleporting tracks upward through a climbing tunnel, the air began to vibrate with a faint, rhythmic thumping. The music grew louder until the tunnel opened into a colossal cavern flooded with neon light.

Peering from the shadows, the party beheld a surreal scene:
*   **Six gray-skinned goblins** clad in polished obsidian glass armor were holding neon glowsticks, throwing a full-blown rave in the center of the cavern.
*   Along the cavern walls were makeshift cages carved into the giant crystals, containing kidnapped citizens from the streets above. 
*   Though Coralai was not in this immediate room, two other tunnels branched deeper into the earth.

Moving like whispers, the party executed a flawless approach. Slipping into perfect tactical positions, they prepared to ambush the dancing goblins. With Kookoush having finally completed Garp's devastating **+2 Anti-Matter Rifle**, the party stood poised to unleash chaos upon the underground rave.
