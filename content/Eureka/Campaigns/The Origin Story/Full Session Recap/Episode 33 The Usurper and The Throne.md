# The Iron Heart and the Mirror of Briars

## Session Overview
* **Location:** Fort Dreadwake (Subterranean Keep & The Iron Tower)
* **Active Party:** Silvia, Slicer, Garp, Kookush, Fynn
* **Key Characters Encountered:** Gawain, Cutter, Edward Teach (The Leader of the Kingdom of Clubs)

---

## Act I: The Furnace Tower and the Secret Eye

The spatial distortion faded, leaving the adventuring party standing within an ancient, sweltering iron chamber beneath **Fort Dreadwake**—a destination reached thanks to **Fynn's** precise knowledge of teleportation circles. 

The heat in the room was oppressive, radiating from deep iron pipes pumping heat from a hidden furnace beneath the floorboards. In the center of the chamber stood a massive Iron Throne, over which hovered a suspended, monstrous **Iron Heart**, beating in a slow, eerie rhythm. Frozen around the base of the dais were ancient skeletons, locked in forever-decayed postures of desperate crawling toward the seat.

While the group debated the throne's obvious curse, **Garp** and **Kookush** took the first cautious steps forward. Blue torchfire suddenly flared to life along the windowless walls, casting cold light over the iron tower. Inspecting the metallic walls, Garp discovered hidden eye-slits—Scooby-Doo-style peep holes built into the thin plating. 

Curious, Garp slid one of the rusted metal plates open with a loud, grinding screech. Peering through, he observed a study containing a portrait of **Edward Teach** and a desk that looked heavily scavenged. Suddenly, heavy, metallic footsteps echoed down the adjacent corridor. 

Garp watched as **Gawain**, the satyr warrior from Cutter's crew, rounded the corner. Gawain’s armor bore a dark, ominous sheen, and his chest glowed red-hot. Gawain scanned the room, checked beneath the desk, and let out a guttural huff before departing. 

Garp quietly reported his findings to the group: someone dangerous was patrolling the keep, and the back of the Iron Throne bore a sinister, familiar crest—a dragon entangled in a ring of red briars.

```
       [ Iron Throne ]
              |
      (Beating Iron Heart)
              |
 [ Anti-Magic Field Generator ]
```

---

## Act II: The Glass Pavilion and the War of Clubs

Determined to move before they were cornered, the party found a hidden latch behind a steel-backed painting in the study, stepping through into the stone corridors of the keep. **Kookush** noted that the architecture was a strange fusion of Eladrin whimsy and dwarven functionality—stone that felt alive, yet carved for cold utility.

Calling out into the hall, **Slicer** intercepted Gawain as the satyr came charging back. Channeling pure charisma and silver-tongued misdirection, Slicer convinced Gawain that they had simply "walked in," deftly bypassing questions about security. Gawain, begrudgingly accepting Slicer's explanations, agreed to lead them to **Cutter**.

Gawain escorted the party out of the stone keep and into an astonishing sight: a massive subterranean dome spanning miles beneath the earth, complete with farmland, stone houses, and an artificial sun sustained by high magic. 

He led them to a grand pavilion constructed of marble and glass. Inside stood a council of formidable figures:
* **Edward Teach**, leader of the Kingdom of Clubs and Garp’s patron.
* **Cutter**, Slicer’s brother.
* A entourage of enforcers, including Bonnie, Reed, the Shader-Kai twins, Vetus, Borson, Lancelot, and Tristol.

Slicer greeted his brother warmly and even threw his arms around the stoic, dangerous Edward Teach. However, the mood quickly darkened as Teach and Cutter laid out their grand design. They were preparing for total war—not merely to defeat Happily Ever After, but to completely obliterate every kingdom across Eureka, tearing down wall and spire alike to return the realm to a brutal survival-of-the-fittest wilderness.

As Teach spoke, a subtle compulsion washed over the onlookers. Garp felt the pull of Teach’s ideals, while Slicer began hearing a static-laced voice in his head, masquerading as his patron, **Morgana**. 

Focusing his mind, Slicer cut through the psychic interference and reached the *real* Morgana. Her voice broke through the illusion in fractured fragments:

> *"Nymiu... sword... throne... sit in the throne!"*

Noticing that both Teach and Cutter carried strange, glass-like blades wrapped in red briars, Slicer realized his brother and the warlord were being subtly manipulated by an outside entity.

---

## Act III: The Anti-Magic Engine

Excusing themselves under the guise of an urgent, collective bathroom break, the party slipped away from the pavilion and doubled back to the hidden chamber of the Iron Throne.

The group analyzed the hovering Iron Heart. Kookush confirmed that a layer of dust was freshly missing from the seat—someone, likely Teach, had sat there recently. 

Trusting Morgana's cryptic directive, Slicer stepped forward. 
"Put your weapons up to my head if you must," Slicer said, "but I'm sitting down."

As Slicer settled onto the cold metal seat, a agonizing surge of necrotic energy ripped through his body. His muscles locked tight; his hands clamped onto the hilt of his sword. Above him, the Iron Heart began to beat at a frenzied pace. 

The heat vanished instantly as the room went ice-cold. The torches flickered violently, turning from red to blue and back again. Suddenly, a massive wave of **Anti-Magic** erupted from the throne, pulsing outward across the subterranean domain.

* Garp’s magical goggles shuttered into plain glass.
* Silvia’s Vorpal Sword lost its radiant shimmer.
* Spells vanished from the minds of the casters.

Slicer suffered intense necrotic strain, his life force acting as the conduit to keep the pulse active. Recognizing the sacrifice, the party realized the purpose of the anti-magic field: **it was strong enough to sever the magical mind-control holding Cutter and Teach in thrall.**

---

## Act IV: The Mirror of Briars

While Kookush, Fynn, and Garp remained in the chamber to monitor Slicer and maintain the perimeter, **Silvia** took advantage of the anti-magic surge and sprinted back toward the glass pavilion. 

With the magic of the realm momentarily deadened, the hypnotic hold over Cutter and Teach snapped. The two leaders stood in the center of the pavilion, suddenly blinking in disoriented confusion as their grand war plans faded like a fever dream.

Silvia rushed forward, targeting the source of the corruption: the glass-like briar rapiers resting at the hips of Cutter and Teach. Disarming the confused men, Silvia cast the crystalline blades to the floor and brought her boot down with full force to shatter them.

Instead of splintering into glass shards, the blades melted instantly into a dark, viscous liquid, pooling together on the pavilion floor. 

Silvia looked down into the dark puddle. Reflected in the liquid was not her own face, but the visage of a **blue Eladrin wearing a crown of woven red briars**. The entity looked up at Silvia through the pool and offered a slow, sinister smile.

## Vault Notes & Key Takeaways

* **The Iron Throne's Function:** Acts as an engine for the Iron Heart. Sitting in it channels the user's vitality to unleash an island-wide Anti-Magic Field.
* **The True Enemy:** Cutter and Edward Teach were not acting entirely of their own free will; they are being puppeted by a mysterious **Blue Eladrin** associated with the dragon and briar symbol.
* **Current Status:** Slicer is injured from the throne's necrotic drain; Silvia has confronted the entity's reflection in the melted briar blades; Teach and Cutter are temporarily freed from mind control.
