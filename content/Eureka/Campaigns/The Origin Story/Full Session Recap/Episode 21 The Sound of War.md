# Campaign Recap: Into the Sands of Rumor

## I. The Bitter Aftermath

The smoke of battle cleared, leaving the party standing amidst the splintered, warped ruins of Mother’s forest. Their "totally-not-evil" boss, Piper, had utterly betrayed them, attempting to detonate a reality-warping bomb that would have undone them all. Though they had managed to destroy the device and survive the subsequent onslaught of corrupted fauna, the victory felt hollow.

*   **A Solemn Vow:** Nursing his wounds from a punishing Fae contract, **Slicer** swore a solemn oath to his companions: *no more deals.* The party, heavily skeptical but appreciative, quickly canonized the promise.
*   **The Wrath of Ursula:** The sea-dwelling Fae, **Ursula**, was furious. Despite the party’s desperate pleas that they had tried to stop the eco-terrorist Piper, Ursula saw only mortal violence and ruin. Declaring she would do whatever was necessary to protect her oceans, she retreated into her bubbling cauldron, leaving a heavy silence in her wake.
*   **A Mother's Grace:** **Mother**, the ancient hag of the woods, did not blame the adventurers. She thanked Slicer for protecting her daughter. As she spoke, a sudden, chilling breeze swept through Slicer’s soul—his temporary pact with Ursula had officially concluded.

---

## II. The Cauldron of Minds

With their employer revealed as a maniacal terrorist, the party faced a grim reality: they still carried invasive tracking crystals, implanted by Happily Ever After, deep within their minds and chests. Desperate to be free of this leverage, they begged Mother to extract them.

### Slicer's Dark Bargain
Mother warned that the extraction would be excruciating and highly dangerous. Fearing for his life, Slicer slipped outside to commune with his patron—the sentient, cursed stone sword fused to his hand. 
*   **The Pact:** In exchange for surviving the extraction, Slicer granted his patron control of his body for **one hour every week**, at a time of the sword's choosing, under the condition that she would not harm his friends. 
*   Upon sealing the deal, Slicer’s flesh temporarily hardened into living, grey stone.

### The Extraction Ritual
One by one, the party climbed into Mother’s bubbling cauldron as she stirred a concoction of shifting purples and greens, chanting in an ancient tongue. The cost of liberty was steep:

*   **Slicer** was pulled under and emerged dazed, suffering from severe arcane withdrawal. For two days, his mind remained entirely clouded (disadvantage on perception and initiative).
*   **Kakush** suffered a brutal psychic backlash (10 psychic damage) but barely managed to hold onto his sanity.
*   **Finn** endured a similar mental tearing (13 psychic damage), emerging deeply disoriented.
*   **Garp**, arriving late to the procedure, was pulled under the boiling liquid and knocked completely unconscious by the psychic trauma (29 psychic damage) before being resuscitated by Sylvia’s healing light.

```
[ Mental Recovery Log: Days 1–4 ]
- Day 1: Slicer and Kakush wake up with total amnesia, convinced they are simple, senile farmers. Finn bullies them mercilessly.
- Day 2: Sylvia installs baby gates and child-proof locks, leaving a note: "You are sick. Go to bed." Slicer tries to pick the lock and fails miserably.
- Day 3: Garp wakes up completely amnesiac, joining the "psych ward" of confused adventurers.
- Day 4: The fog slowly begins to clear, though Kakush and Finn remain highly regressed and prone to casting random spells.
```

By the fifth day, the party’s minds were finally clear. But the cost of their cure was written plainly on their host.

---

## III. The Rooting of Mother

Mother had paid for the party's cure with her own ebbing vitality. She had aged eighty years in a matter of days, her skin wrinkly, her frame hunched and fragile. She looked like a classic, dying hag.

*   **The Last Walk:** Mother requested to be taken deep into the woods to a black, bare-limbed tree. Sylvia and Garp carried her frail form, with Slicer following close behind.
*   **The Awakening:** At her touch, the dead tree uprooted itself, its bark shifting into a humanoid face. The great tree-spirit gently cradled Mother. 
*   **A Final Transformation:** Mother whispered her fears of what Ursula and her sisters would do when they realized she was gone. As the tree-spirit carried her back to the cottage, Mother’s skin began to harden into dark bark. She sat in her favorite chair, her lower half slowly taking root, fusing permanently with the wood of her home.

Leaving a quiet note of gratitude, the party walked away from the cottage, leaving the ancient matriarch to her eternal rest.

---

## IV. Downriver to the Dunes

Returning to the lake, the party found their suspicions confirmed: the Happily Ever After airship was gone, reclaimed by their former employers. They were stranded.

*   **The Driftwood Raft:** Relying on Garp’s nautical expertise and some high-quality survival instincts, the party constructed a sturdy raft from fallen limbs and rope.
*   **The Shifting Wilds:** They floated down the river, watching the landscape rapidly evolve. The dense forest melted into vast grasslands, which quickly parched, dried, and dissolved into the endless, blinding sands of a massive desert.
*   **The Border Village:** They docked their raft at a small settlement of thirty buildings inhabited by sandy-skinned elves. Beyond the village lay the *Sands of Rumor*, and somewhere within them, the nomads who held clues to Garp’s missing family.

---

## V. Preparations in the Borderlands

Crossing the desert on foot was a death sentence. The party immediately set about trading their tainted corporate gear for survival equipment.

### The Slend Sinker's Forge
Slicer marched into the local forge, run by a loosely-draped, sandy-skinned elf. 
*   **The Trade:** Slicer sold his branded Happily Ever After half-plate for a cool 400 gold pieces, purchasing a breathable breastplate, desert goggles, and a protective scarf.
*   **The Artificer's Project:** Garp sought to upgrade his anti-matter rifle to a +2 model. Learning the local smith would take a week and 2500 gold, the party opted to buy 1500 gold worth of rare alloys and diamond dust. **Kakush**, their resident artificer, agreed to upgrade the rifle manually during their travels.

### Braving the Heat
To survive the oppressive desert sun, the party made two crucial purchases:
1.  **Desert Clothing:** Loose, breathable garb to protect them from extreme heat.
2.  **The Frost Stone:** A rare magical artifact that projects a radius of refreshing, icy air. Through a combination of Slicer’s shameless flirting and a flash of genius, they haggled the shopkeeper down to 1000 gold, splitting the cost to keep their armored members from baking alive.

### The Lizard Breeder
Just outside the village, they located a tattered dwarf who bred massive, sandy-colored desert lizards—beasts resembling giant, four-legged bearded dragons built for traversing dunes.

*   **Finn's Unspeakable Bargain:** Lacking the 200 gold required for a mount, Finn negotiated a "bargain of unspeakable personal indignity" with the lonely dwarf behind closed doors. He emerged holding the keys to a fine red-and-brown lizard, leaving his companions shielding their eyes in silent judgment.
*   **Slicer's High Stakes:** Slicer challenged the dwarf to a double-or-nothing coin flip, ultimately winning a second lizard for 230 gold.
*   **"Old Rusty":** Kakush purchased a third lizard—a incredibly old, slow-moving beast named Old Rusty—for a discounted 75 gold.

```
[ The Sandsled Rig ]
Using the driftwood raft from their river journey, Garp's spare shield as metal skis, and fifty feet of rope, the party constructed a makeshift sand-sled. 

Harnessed to their three giant desert lizards (including the painfully slow Old Rusty), the sled was perfectly positioned within the cooling radius of the Frost Stone.
```

With their makeshift sand-sled packed, their water skins filled, and their giant lizards hissed in unison, the party turned their faces toward the shimmering horizon. The *Sands of Rumor* awaited.
