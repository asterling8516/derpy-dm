# Chronicle of the Oasis Siege: Shadows, Steel, and Shattered Portals

## Prologue: The Cost of Victory
The dust of the under-labyrinth had barely settled. The goblin king, Jix, was dead, his treasures plundered, and the long-suffering Guillermo had finally drawn his last breath. For **Garp**, victory was immediately punctuated by a sharp, stinging slap across the face—a tense reunion with his estranged wife, Coraline, who was entirely unreceptive to his thoughts on child support. 

With the hostages rescued and a goblin thoroughly interrogated via battery by **Silvia**, the party used Ben’s *Wand of Secrets* to navigate back to the surface. They returned to Oasis City only to find its populace on the verge of a grotesque transformation into goblins. At the Neon Canopy, the bartender, Prism, pointed them toward Grimhold as their only hope for a cure. 

Trading Jix’s tablet and information to Theron, the half-orc militia captain, in exchange for free passage to Grimhold in two days, the party broke up for some well-deserved downtime. **Fynn** rode a wave of spectacular luck at the gambling tables, turning his gold into a staggering 830 gold pieces, while **Kookush** and **Garp** lost their stakes almost instantly. Still, they walked away with impressive spoils: Garp sported stylish new sunglasses and Jix’s crown, while Kookush claimed three mana gems and Jix's powerful scepter.

## Chapter 1: Plans, Potions, and Prototypes
With two days of downtime ahead, the party gathered in the stables on the outskirts of Oasis City to prepare for the journey ahead. 

**Kookush** began by identifying a strange, tar-like substance salvaged from the goblin king's quarters. After a brief ritual, he determined it to be a thick, black potion. Drinking it would grant resistance to necrotic damage for an hour, though its peanut-butter-like consistency would render the drinker entirely mute for the duration. 

Turning his attention to crafting, Kookush offered his services to the party. With **Silvia’s** assistance, he set to work on a custom shoulder-mount for Garp’s devastating anti-matter rifle. Melting down a spare set of chainmail to cover the material costs, Kookush masterfully fashioned a reinforced shoulder pad on his own gear, allowing Garp to stand behind him in combat, steady his aim, and gain a tactical advantage on his shots. 

Meanwhile, Kookush began researching the components needed for a *Cloak of Protection*, earmarking 300 gold pieces for rare materials and planning a trip into the city's markets.

## Chapter 2: Echoes of Stone and Steel
That night, as the party slept, **Slicer** drifted into a vivid, haunting dream. 

He found himself standing in a subterranean city carved entirely of stone. Around him were petrified citizens frozen in moments of mundane panic—parents holding their children's hands, merchants carrying stone supplies. Slicer’s feet moved of their own accord, drawing him toward a grand castle bearing a stone banner: a dragon with a circle of briars around its neck. 

Inside the empty, ornate throne room stood a noble Eladrin woman. She possessed sharp features, piercing emerald eyes, and hair like autumn gold, draped in long green cloaks. At her side was a staff. 

"Please, sit with me," she said, conjuring a stone table and chairs. 

She introduced herself as **Morgana**. She spoke of history repeating itself, warning Slicer that his brother was currently being hunted by the same entity that had destroyed her kingdom. For the first time, Slicer felt the constant, cursed grip of his sword loosen; he could let go if he wished. Instead, he chose to sit upon the empty throne.

Morgana waved her hands, rewinding the stone room to a time of vibrant life. Slicer watched a grand court of Eladrin, ruled by a king who looked remarkably like Slicer’s own father. Beside the king stood Morgana, and next to her was a woman of pale skin, flowing blue hair, and robes of living water: **Nimu**. 

"Nimu was once my friend," Morgana whispered. "But she corrupted my king."

The memory turned violent. Nimu grabbed a massive sword and drove it through the king's heart. As Morgana screamed in anguish, a terrible wave of petrification washed over the kingdom, turning flesh to stone. The feedback of the magic began to unravel Morgana's body, but a transparent, ancient Eladrin separated Morgana's soul from her dying flesh, binding her spirit to the very blade that slew the king. Nimu, attempting to flee by turning to water, was caught in the petrifying wave and frozen into a strange, hollow glass form. The transparent Eladrin then hurled the soul-bound sword out of the castle, casting it into the far reaches of the world.

"Your brother has found her," Morgana warned. "He is to be her next plaything. The only way to save him is to destroy the sword that binds them."

Slicer reached out and took Morgana's hand, accepting her warning and her pact. The connection to his blade surged with newfound power. Slicer woke in his stable bed, his grip firm on his weapon, hearing her voice echo in his mind: *“Nice to meet you, Slicer.”*

## Chapter 3: The Shadows of Seraphina
On the second day of downtime, the party prepared to venture into the city markets. Before they left, Fynn lingered in the stables. Channeling his devotion to Lady Seraphina, he carved her likeness into the wooden walls. As he finished, the shadows of the stable unnaturally stretched and wrapped around him, cloaking him in a protective shroud of darkness. 

Nearby, Silvia's magical flower began to emit a foul, putrid stench. Using it like a dowsing rod, she pointed it toward the heart of Oasis City. The rot was spreading.

The party made their way into the bustling city to secure crafting supplies. Fynn purchased materials to carve twenty small pocket-sized idols of Seraphina, while Kookush gathered the components for his future cloak. 

However, the atmosphere in the city was deeply unsettling. **Garp**, keeping a watchful eye on the crowds, noticed dozens of hooded figures moving through the streets. Peering beneath their cloaks, his sharp eyes realized the horrifying truth: these were not people. They were *Happily Ever After* automatons disguised with crude, painted clay skin.

---

## Chapter 4: Project Unity Unleashed
Before the party could warn the local authorities, a booming, magically amplified voice echoed from the massive *Happily Ever After* blimp hovering near the city's ziggurat. 

> *"Congratulations, Oasis City! You have been chosen for the breaking ground of Project Unity!"*

A canned track of laughter and applause boomed over the streets. Instantly, the disguised automatons tore off their cloaks, revealing their cold, metallic bodies. Their chests popped open, and they retrieved small, brass gramophones, holding them high to the sky. 

> *"Let the unification begin."*

The gramophones detonated in a synchronized, magical chain reaction. Violent concussive force rippled through the streets, tearing down buildings and ripping open unstable, swirling portals. The rifts began violently sucking the screaming citizens of Oasis City into the void. 

Reacting with lightning speed, the party dove for cover. Fynn tackled a fleeing woman, shielding her from the gravitational pull of a nearby rift. But around them, dozens of innocent people were dragged into the portals, only for horrifying, pitch-black ooze beasts to begin crawling out of the rifts in their place. 

Recognizing the magic of the void, Fynn made a desperate, reckless decision. "They didn't choose this!" he roared, sprinting headlong into the nearest portal. 

Fynn emerged on the other side in a blinding, white void—the chaotic plane of Pandemonium. Hundreds of portals hung in the empty space, pulling in screaming mortals and violently ripping them apart, reassembling their flesh into the black ooze beasts. 

Calling upon the chaotic god Talos and burning through every ounce of his magical reserves, Fynn began slamming his hands onto the portals, forcing them shut. The magical feedback was agonizing, tearing at his physical form, but he held his ground, desperately sealing the rifts one by one.

---

## Chapter 5: Blood in the Streets of Oasis
Back in the material plane, Oasis City had descended into a warzone. Armed with the knowledge that Fynn was fighting from the inside, the rest of the party rallied. The local militia, led by Theron, joined the fray, drawing their weapons to defend the surviving citizens.

* **Slicer** lunged into the horde. Though he initially tripped over a fleeing commoner, he recovered with terrifying grace, his blade flashing in a deadly arc that cleaved several beasts in half.
* **Silvia** became a whirlwind of death. Brandishing her *Vorpal Sword* and channeling her psionic strikes, she sliced through the creatures with brutal efficiency, her blade echoing with sonic booms.
* **Garp** took up his position behind Kookush. Resting the heavy barrel of his anti-matter rifle on Kookush’s newly crafted shoulder pad, he unleashed devastating, split-beam blasts that vaporized rows of beasts. When the monsters closed the distance, Garp slammed Jix’s scepter into the cobblestones, releasing a blinding shockwave of energy that left the surrounding horde dazed and vulnerable.
* **Kookush** summoned a roaring *Flaming Sphere*, sending it rolling down the avenues to incinerate the advancing monsters while picking off stragglers with his *Bio-Bolt Caster*.

In the white void of Pandemonium, Fynn was reaching his absolute limit. With only a few portals remaining, he gathered the last of his strength, channeling a spectacular surge of wild magic to seal the final rifts. As the last portal began to collapse, Fynn dove back through the tearing fabric of reality, tumbling onto the blood-slicked streets of Oasis City. 

The magical backlash was catastrophic. Fynn’s body shriveled, steam rising from his scorched skin as he collapsed into unconsciousness. Silvia rushed to his side, administering healing hands, while Garp shouted a *Healing Word* to pull their friend back from the brink of death.

With the portals closed and the horde decimated by the combined might of the party and the militia, the remaining ooze beasts were swiftly hunted down. Above, the *Happily Ever After* blimp went dark, its flashing advertisements shutting off as it retreated silently into the clouds.

---

## Chapter 6: A Cold Reunion
Oasis City was left in ruins. Smoldering rubble, shattered streets, and the cries of the wounded filled the air. 

Battered, exhausted, and bleeding, the party bypassed the gathering crowds and rushed toward the safehouse where Coraline and Caspian were hidden. When they arrived, their hearts sank. A portion of the building had collapsed into a now-closed portal. Dead ooze beasts littered the perimeter. 

Slicer, Garp, and Kookush scanned the ground, spotting a trail of frantic footsteps leading deeper into the ruined structure, disappearing behind a partially shattered door. 

Garp stepped forward, pushing the door open. 

Instantly, a figure blurred from the shadows. Before Garp could react, the cold, sharp edge of a dagger was pressed firmly against his throat. 

Coraline stood before him, her eyes wild with adrenaline and fury.
