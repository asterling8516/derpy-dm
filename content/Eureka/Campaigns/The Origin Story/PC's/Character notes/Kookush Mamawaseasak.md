# Kookoush Mamakwasesak - Notes
## Backstory
Self exiled from his small town of elven kind, nestled between the highlands and forest. The people there were mainly farmers and druids, living off the land and harvesting it's bounty. Nature was revered above all and Kookoush's propensity to use nature's bounty for his own pleasures was met with scorn.

He left and headed to the largest city within a few days travel, a middling city of no real significance where nothing really stood out and caught the eye. Perfect.

Several years later, he had established himself a comfortable but humble life, a simple green cottage with brown trim to call home and passing most of the day away as a regular in one of the cities taverns. The Murky Drum was second favorite of everyday laborers, skilled tradesmen and lower class townies and therefore busy enough but never crowded. To a traveler, rare patron or the unaware he would look no more than a dull local but his bathrobe and bunny slippers suggest a tenuous grasp on logic and long conversations to be avoided about 'how they faked the Feywild landings.' But within these wall he sold his wares, a small cut going to the tavern owner to look the other way.

Originally he sold poultices, healing salves, recovery soups, potions and elixirs. When he noticed some of his patrons had more of a sickness of the soul than body, he began to trade from his own personal stockpile of 'entertainments.'

Fortunately this city wasn't big enough or important enough to have much of a underground society, despite his best efforts to remain in the scenery he was ratted out by small time competitor to the city watch and imprisoned. There he met the curiosity, the irritation and 'the help' ... a small halfling by the name of Watt. Calling himself a barrister, and wielding charisma.

## Personality Traits
The first thing I do in a new place is note the locations of everything valuable—or where such things could be hidden.

**Ideals:** People. I'm loyal to my friends, not to any ideals, and everyone else can take a trip down the Styx for all I care.

**Bonds:** To prove his home villagers wrong.

**Flaws:** My bravado or confidence is a front, a lie I tell myself and show others as I'd prefer to keep distance and watch from afar. I don't like to lead, nor really converse much unless my trade is involved.
