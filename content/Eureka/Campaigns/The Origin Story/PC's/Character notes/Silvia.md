# Silvia Surath - Notes
## Backstory
A former angel of death who regrets her inaction to stop sources of unfair death. She has rebelled against heaven and lost almost all of her powers but stands firm in her quest to slay those that inflict unfair death on the innocent.

## Personality Traits
**Personality Trait:** I refuse to become a victim, and I will not allow others to be victimized.
**Ideals:** I kill monsters to make the world a safer place, and to exorcise my own demons. (Good)
**Bonds:** A terrible guilt consumes me. I hope that I can find redemption through my actions.
**Flaws:** I am a purveyor of doom and gloom who lives in a world without hope.
