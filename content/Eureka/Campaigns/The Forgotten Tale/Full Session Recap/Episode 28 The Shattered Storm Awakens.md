Having carved a vertical shaft down through the solid ice of the Stillwater Shards, the party found themselves standing before a awe-inspiring sight: an ancient, massive dragon frozen mid-fall within crystal-clear ice. Its scales shimmered strangely, shifting between radiant white and stormy blue.

### A Thousand-Year Thaw

Determined to speak with the giant beast without risking an immediate rampage, **Odin** and **Frezer** channeled their magical abilities to carefully dispel the ice encasing the dragon's head. As the magic broke, a violent gust of arctic wind erupted into the chamber, coating the party in frost before the spellcasters managed to suppress the storm.

With the dragon's snout freed, the party attempted to stir the ancient leviathan:
* **Bearksin** and **Odin** stepped forward to pet the dragon’s snout, only to be blasted with static bursts of frost and lightning that bit through their armor.
* Recalling ancient lore, **Skal** and **Frezer** realized this creature had likely been entombed here for over a thousand years, dating back to the grand planar conflict when Tiamat sealed the portals.
* **Odin**, ever resourceful, set up a makeshift camp stove directly beneath the frozen dragon and began roasting seasoned meat, fanning the savory scent toward its nostrils in hopes of awakening its appetite.

When physical touching failed to arouse the creature from its deep slumber, **Skal** cast *Sending* directly into its mind. Instead of a lucid response, Skal was met with terrifying psychic screams echoing in the dragon's nightmare: *"Tiamat, no! The Hells are coming! It's getting cold!"*

Recognizing the creature was severely weakened, **Bearksin** forced healing potions into the dragon's partially open jaw. As the restorative magic coursed through its veins, the dullness faded from its scales, and its massive eyes fluttered. **Skal** reached out mentally once more, shouting across the dreamscape: *"Tiamat is gone! You have to wake up!"*

### Panic and Persuasion

The words shattered the dragon's thousand-year trance. Erupting from its stupor with a panic-stricken roar, the dragon unleashed a torrent of frost and lightning across the subterranean chamber. 

* The breath weapon caught the party off guard, searing them with elemental energy.
* In her disoriented rage, the dragon snapped forward, biting **Odin** and crushing him against the icy cavern floor.
* **Adamson** immediately laid down his weapons, channeling a wave of soothing *Mass Cure Wounds* magic from his staff to heal his allies while calling out in defense: *"We are here to help you! The war ended a thousand years ago!"*

Seeing the mortals lay down their arms and hearing their desperate pleas, the dragon stayed her claws. The reality of her situation hit the ancient creature all at once—her kin were gone, her realm was transformed, and an industrialized empire known as *Happily Ever After* was currently drilling into her tomb to harvest her power. Overwhelmed by grief, the great beast collapsed into heavy, tearful sobs, sending localized bursts of lightning and snow swirling through the cave. 

**Adamson** stepped forward to comfort the weeping behemoth, while **Odin** quietly went back to roasting meat to offer a small token of peace.

### Eruption into the Sky

Realizing that the *Happily Ever After* mining fleets were rapidly closing in on the cavern, the party explained their situation. The dragon looked up at the tight vertical shaft the adventurers had dug and offered a alternative escape route: *"Climb aboard."*

**Skal**, **Bearksin**, **Odin**, **Frezer**, and **Adamson** scrambled onto the massive dragon's back. Shaking her wings, the dragon gathered elemental power, causing the entire cavern to tremble. With a mighty thrust, she launched upward, shattering the frozen ceiling of the island entirely. 

The party erupted into the open air amidst a shower of ice, obliterated mining carts, and falling automatons. Suspended above them was the massive *Happily Ever After* flagship, surrounded by a fleet of black war blimps.

### Aerial Skirmish

Two armed blimps immediately broke formation to intercept the escaping leviathan:
* The dragon wheeled mid-air and opened her maw, unleashing a devastating storm cone of pure lightning and ice. The frost-blast struck the first blimp dead-on, tearing a colossal chunk out of its hull and sending it plunging down toward the sea.
* As the second blimp closed in, **Frezer** and **Skal** combined their offensive magic, hurling a devastating *Fireball* into the vessel's gas envelope. The blimp erupted into flames, drifting helplessly away from the battle.

With the skies temporarily cleared, the dragon swooped low toward the shoreline, depositing the party safely near their own airship. As the massive creature prepared to take flight into the clouds, she looked back at the mortals who had freed her.

*"I was once known as Azuriel, the Shattered Storm,"* she boomed over the wind, before soaring off into the horizon, leaving the heroes to face the gathering armada above.
