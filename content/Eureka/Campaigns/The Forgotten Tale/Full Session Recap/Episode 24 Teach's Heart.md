# The Shadow Over Dread Lake

An uneasy silence hung over the deck of the party's airship as it hovered near the outskirts of Shadow's Haven. The world was fracturing; planes were on the verge of collision, and the devastating news of Oasis City’s destruction weighed heavily on everyone's minds. 

Before them lay two perilous paths:
*   **Option A:** Venture to Infernium and infiltrate the tower within *Happily Ever After* to rescue their ally, Codex.
*   **Option B:** Search for the legendary **Astral Focus** at **Fort Dread Lake**, a massive stronghold rumored to have been built by a Fae prince and currently occupied by the infamous pirate lord, Teach.

With **Adamson** bound to an Archfey pact and **Bearksin**’s very life force mystically tethered to him, the stakes were incredibly high. After a tense vote, the scales tipped. The party resolved to strike at the closer evil: Fort Dread Lake.

---

## Horror on the Wind

As the airship uncloaked and charted a course southward, **Odin**, **Skal**, and **Adamson** kept watch from the bow. The sky grew crowded. A desperate flotilla of nearly forty vessels—cargo ships, passenger skiffs, and beast-drawn carriages—was fleeing the ruin of Oasis City, seeking refuge in Shadow's Haven. 

Suddenly, the sky erupted in chaos. 

From the deck of one of the crowded cargo ships, massive, oily black tentacles burst forth, dragging screaming refugees into the dark. While some argued to steer clear, the party's conscience won out. **Frezer** steered the airship parallel to the panicked vessel, bringing them within striking distance.

At the center of the cargo deck, a red-haired woman named Coraline clung to her young son, Caspian. In her hand, she held a strange mirror etched with the symbol of a library—a book crossed by a quill and sword. Through the glass, a shifting, infinite library could be seen. Coraline explained through tears that her husband, Garf, and his companions had been dragged into the mirror by the tentacles. 

Realizing the mirror was a powerful scrying tool linked to the Nexus Library, and recognizing the danger of the tentacles returning, **Frezer** used a stern hand to convince Coraline and Caspian to board their airship. The party quickly dropped the mother and child off at the relative safety of Shadow's Haven before pushing their engines to the limit, racing toward Fort Dread Lake.

---

## Fort Dread Lake and the Hidden Path

The fortress was a terrifying sight. Shaped like a massive, five-pointed star of ancient stone, it spanned acres of the island's interior. A colossal stone tower jutted from its center, bearing an ancient, weathered crest: a dragon entwined in sharp briars.

The party landed on the island's shoreline, camouflaging their ship once more. Using the clues provided by Yorick, **Skal** located a peculiar, unnatural copse of trees. 

The scout, Thalen, slipped through the tree line cloaked in invisibility, but when he failed to return, **Bearksin** stepped through the threshold. The barrier was a teleportation portal. Bearksin materialized inside a cold, dark stone tunnel, nearly knocking over an waiting Thalen. The rest of the party quickly followed.

```
                  [ Shoreline Portal ]
                           |
                           v
              [ Trapped Stone Tunnel ]
                           |
                           v
             [ Chamber of the Iron Heart ]
```

As they crept deeper into the subterranean passage, **Skal** spotted raised stones on the floor and protruding bricks in the walls—pressure plates designed to flood the corridor with toxic gas. Deciding these traps might serve as a useful defense if they had to retreat, the party carefully bypassed them, leaving them fully armed.

---

## The Beating Heart and the Iron Throne

The stone corridor eventually gave way to massive iron supports, opening into a breathtaking, eighty-foot-tall vault. The air here was stiflingly hot. Suspended in the center of the room was a massive, metallic heart, beating with a slow, heavy rhythm. Beneath it sat a rusted throne of solid iron, surrounded by the brittle, hollowed-out bones of those who had dared trespass before.

Driven by curiosity and a reckless bravado, **Bearksin** marched up and sat upon the iron throne. 

Instantly, the chamber reacted:
*   The metallic heart's beat accelerated into a frantic thumping.
*   The party's magical lanterns died, plunging the room into absolute, non-magical darkness.
*   The oppressive heat vanished, replaced by a sudden, draining chill.
*   An anti-magic field washed over the room. **Bearksin** suffered a devastating wave of necrotic energy, his mystical *Eyes of the Eagle* failed, and his clockwork companion collapsed, lifeless.

Feeling his strength rapidly fading, Bearksin scrambled off the throne. Immediately, the heart slowed, the heat returned, and the party's magical items flickered back to life.

---

## Secrets of the Office

Desperate to avoid the throne room, **Odin** discovered a sliding metal peephole disguised behind a painting on the wall. Peering through, they saw a lavishly decorated office. Sprawled across the floor were several sleeping giants—grotesque, multi-limbed, multi-headed abominations belonging to Teach’s crew. 

The party quietly slid the painting aside and slipped into the room. While the abominations slept, **Adamson** searched a grand wooden desk. He uncovered:
*   Childish crayon drawings of superheroes fighting a three-headed dragon, signed by two names: *"Slicer"* and *"Cutter"*.
*   A beautiful, cursed dagger made of glass, its hilt wrapped in red briars.

When **Frezer** picked up the glass dagger to identify it, the briars on the hilt began to writhe, wrapping around his hand as dark whispers flooded his mind. Shaking off the curse, Frezer dropped the weapon in horror. Not wanting to leave a magical asset behind, **Skal** swept the entire drawer—dagger, drawings, and all—into his *Bag of Holding*.

---

## An Unholy Alliance

Thalen slipped out of the office to scout the rest of the fortress. When he returned, he was pale and breathing heavily. 

"Teach is here," Thalen whispered. "And it's worse than we thought."

The pirate lord and his crew had confronted the abominations in the courtyard. But instead of a bloodbath, a truce had been struck. The giants had presented Teach with a magnificent, briar-wrapped glass rapier—a sister weapon to the dagger in Skal's bag. The alliance was sealed. Worse, Thalen suspected the Astral Focus was already gone, likely delivered into the hands of the Archfey, Celeste.

With their primary objective seemingly out of reach and a small army of pirates and mutated giants patrolling the grounds, escape was the only logical choice. However, the main gate—a massive iron portcullis and drawbridge—was heavily guarded by five grotesque abominations, including conjoined twins and a two-headed giant.

---

## The Distraction Plan

Huddled in the shadows of the blacksmith's shop, the party formulated a desperate plan to exploit the kegs of ghost powder they had purchased.

```
                    [ The Gate ]
                   /     |      \
                  /      |       \
                 /       |        \
       [Left Barrel]     |     [Right Barrel]
       (Skal & Adamson)  |     (Odin & Frezer)
                         v
                  [Gate Control]
                (Bearksin & Thalen)
```

1.  **The Left Flank:** **Skal** and **Adamson** will sneak to the left side of the courtyard to plant and prime a keg of ghost powder.
2.  **The Right Flank:** **Odin** and **Frezer** will do the same on the right.
3.  **The Trigger:** Once in position, they will detonate the barrels simultaneously using *Firebolt*, creating a massive, chaotic diversion to split the guards.
4.  **The Breach:** While the giants investigate the explosions, Thalen and **Bearksin** will slip into the guard tower to raise the gate, allowing the party to sprint to freedom.

With their weapons checked and their hearts pounding in tandem with the iron heart below, the party prepared to light the fuses...
