### Thawing the Sleeper

Deep beneath the Stillwater Shards, the companions stood in an ancient cavern before an impossible sight: a colossal blue dragon suspended mid-fall, encased entirely in clear, magical ice. Its scales shimmered strangely, shifting between brilliant cobalt and glacial white.

Determined to make contact without immediately inviting a draconic rampage, **Frezer** and **Odin** stepped forward. Combining their artifice and spellcraft, they cast *Dispel Magic* with surgical precision, thawing only the ice surrounding the dragon’s massive head while leaving the jaw locked and the body caged. A burst of icy wind lashed out at them as the seal cracked, but Odin managed to push the storm away.

While the beast remained unconscious, the party attempted various methods to rouse it:
* **Odin** built a campfire directly on the cavern floor and began cooking a slab of meat, fanning the savory smoke directly toward the beast’s snout.
* **Bearksin** and **Adamson** attempted to gently boop the snout, only to be blasted by a sudden discharge of static frost shock that left them heavily bruised and shivering.
* **Bearksin** poured potions of healing down the dragon's snout, restoring a vibrant sheen to her dormant scales.

---

### Whispers in the Ice

Seeking a safer line of communication, **Skal** cast *Sending* directly into the dragon's mind. Instead of a lucid response, he was met with a chorus of panicked inner screams echoing across a thousand-year memory: 

> *"Tiamat, no! The Hells are coming! It's getting cold!"*

Realizing the dragon was trapped in a ancient nightmare, **Frezer** and **Odin** proceeded to dispel the remainder of the ice block, freeing her massive form entirely. With the beast free, **Skal** projected another urgent message directly into her consciousness: 

> *"You have to wake up! Tiamat is gone!"*

---

### A Panicked Tempest

The mental shout shattered the slumber, but not the panic. The dragon’s eyes snapped open, clouded with a thousand years of confusion. Believing the battle against the Hells was still waging around her, she opened her jaw and unleashed a lingering breath attack—a roaring tempest of frost and lightning that engulfed the entire chamber.

**Bearksin**, **Adamson**, **Frezer**, **Skal**, and **Odin** scrambled for cover, taking heavy elemental damage. Consumed by confusion, the dragon snapped downward, biting **Odin** viciously.

Seeing the situation spiraling into catastrophe, **Adamson** laid his weapons upon the ground. Speaking with quiet authority and gentle magic, he soothed the dragon's thrashing spirit. **Skal** and the others quickly called out that they were friends who had just freed her from her tomb.

As the fog of sleep cleared from her pale eyes, the reality of her situation set in. The great beast slumped to the cavern floor and began to weep weeping massive tears that sparked with lightning and turned to snow.

---

### Revelations and the Evil Empire

Between her heavy, tearful sobs, the party laid out the stark truth of the modern world:
* Over a thousand years had passed since she fell beneath the ice.
* Tiamat was gone, and the ancient gods were fading.
* A ruthless, corporate syndicate known as **Happily Ever After**—allied with forces from the Hells—was actively mining down through the mountain right above them to harvest her body and power.

**Adamson** did his best to comfort the giant weeping dragon, even draping a tiny blanket over a small patch of her scales. The party assured her they wanted no reward; their only goal was to thwart Happily Ever After and ensure she remained free.

---

### Flight of the Shattered Storm

With the mining operations of Happily Ever After echoing from the tunnels above, the dragon bade the party climb onto her back. 

**Bearksin**, **Adamson**, **Frezer**, **Skal**, and **Odin** secured themselves along her massive spine. With a violent thrust of her wings, the dragon launched upward. The sheer force of her ascent shattered the core of the icy island, obliterating Happily Ever After’s mining automatons and tearing through the mountain peak. 

They erupted into the open air beneath the shadow of a massive Happily Ever After dreadnought hovering above the shards.

---

### Battle Above the Shards

As they burst into the sky, two black Happily Ever After escort airships detached from the main mothership and gave chase, closing the distance rapidly.

The party and their draconic mount turned to fight:
* **The Dragon's Wrath:** Banking hard in mid-air, the blue dragon opened her jaws and unleashed a full-powered breath weapon. A cone of devastating storm-ice and lightning tore through the first escort ship, ripping a massive hole through its hull and sending it plunging to the ocean below.
* **Frezer's Bolt:** **Frezer** hurled a *Javelin of Lightning*, launching a five-foot-wide bolt of electricity that tore through the second airship's structure.
* **Odin's Inferno:** **Odin** raised his hands and launched a roaring *Fireball* directly into the blimp's gas envelope, igniting the canvas in a massive fireball.

Engulfed in flames, the second ship drifted helplessly out of the sky.

---

### Epilogue: The Shattered Storm

With the sky cleared of immediate threats, the great dragon descended gracefully, depositing the weary party safely onto the deck of their own ship. 

Before taking to the open skies to find her place in a world a thousand years ahead of her time, she turned her pale, ancient eyes toward the companions. When asked her name, she answered with a voice like rolling thunder:

> **"I am Azuriel. I was once known as the Shattered Storm."**
