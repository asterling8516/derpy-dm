# The Shadowshaven Heist: Whispers, Gunpowder, and Graves

The skies of the Shadowfell were thick with gloom as the party's airship neared the chaotic, floating docks of **Shadowshaven**. Built upon a jagged island surrounded by swirling mists and uncoordinated vessels, the pirate haven was a lawless sanctuary of thieves, smugglers, and cutthroats. 

---

## Hiding the Vessel

Knowing the high crime rate of the city, the party chose not to dock publicly. To prevent their ship from being stolen by rival crime families, **Frezer** guided the vessel to a quiet landing spot roughly a mile outside the city limits. 

* **A Guardian Left Behind:** The party decided to leave **Daryl**, their trusted helper, aboard to guard the ship. Frezer spent several patient minutes explaining how to use a magical *scroll of sending* in case of an emergency. With a bit of guidance, Daryl nodded in understanding, proving himself more than capable.
* **The Art of Camouflage:** To keep the ship hidden from prying eyes, the party gathered brush and snow. Despite some clumsy interference from **Odin** and **Bearskin**, Frezer managed to successfully drape the vessel in a highly convincing layer of camouflage.

With their escape route secured, the adventurers set off on foot toward the chaotic streets of Shadowshaven.

---

## The Drunken Compass

**Skal** led the party through the winding, shadowed streets to a tavern known as **The Drunken Compass**—a massive, overturned warship converted into a rowdy watering hole. The air inside was thick with the smell of stale ale, roasted meats, and heavy tar. 

* **Frezer’s Grand Entrance:** Pushing through the crowd of Shadar-kai and gray-skinned goblins, Frezer ordered a massive keg of ale and plates of food. The boisterous bartender flung the heavy keg and plates directly over the bar. Showing incredible reflexes, Frezer caught the keg and saved most of the plates, earning cheers from the drunken crowd as he resisted the heavy fumes of the local brew.
* **The Friendly Minotaur:** Bearskin tried to sit with a grim-looking group of tieflings, offering to share his keg. He was promptly rebuffed by their cold, suspicious glares, though he stubbornly remained seated to rest his aching legs.
* **The Arm-Wrestle:** Seeking information, **Adamson** caught the attention of **Ray Lard**, a notorious pirate captain famous for making things "disappear." When Adamson was teased for his short stature, he challenged the pirates to an arm-wrestle. Before the match, a spell was almost cast to aid him, but Ray Lard called out the magic as cheating and demanded Adamson remove his *belt of giant strength*. Without his magical toy, Adamson was instantly pinned. Though he lost two silver coins, the display earned them a private audience with the pirate lord.

---

## A Warning from the Sky

As the party stepped outside with Ray Lard to speak privately, a strange, dry rustling filled the air. Looking up, they saw hundreds of delicate paper owls drifting down through the dark clouds. One of the folded constructs descended directly into their hands.

Unfolding the paper, they read a dire warning from **Codex**:

> *"To all librarians: A faction known as 'Happily Ever After' has breached the archives. They seek an unknown prize and have already eradicated Oasis City to fuel 'Project Unity,' using sound bombs to turn innocent folk into horrific monsters. Retrieve what knowledge you can, return to your libraries, and destroy all evidence of our connection. Humanity is under attack."*

Ray Lard watched the owls descend with narrow eyes, realizing his new companions were tangled in something far larger—and far deadlier—than simple piracy.

---

## An Alliance Bought in Gold

The party followed Ray Lard to his personal vessel, a striking, red-hulled Man of War with black sails. On the deck, surrounded by fifteen of the captain's loyal crew, they negotiated for information regarding the stolen **astrophocus**.

* **The Price of Truth:** For the hefty price of **5,000 gold coins**, Ray Lard revealed what he knew. 
* **The Destination:** A tiefling woman matching the description of the vault thief had been boasting about her arson in Frostlight. She and an army of roughly thirty associates had sailed to **Fort Dreadwake**—an abandoned, isolated fortress built by a Fey prince on a southern island. 
* **The Warning:** Ray Lard warned them that the fortress was currently claimed by the legendary pirate king **Edward** (often called **Teach**), and that storming the front gates would be suicide.

---

## The Keelhaul Market

Hoping to even the odds against a small army, the party headed to the **Keelhaul Market**, a bustling bazaar housed within the massive, skeletal rib cage of an ancient galleon.

* **Specialized Gear:** From a Shadar-kai merchant, they purchased a magical lantern capable of revealing the invisible, alongside three black pearl smoke bombs.
* **A Grim Sight:** Passing the *Jolly Roger Apothecary*, they witnessed patients suffering from **Shadow’s Rot**—a terrifying affliction of the Shadowfell that slowly dissolves a person’s body into living shadow.
* **Ghost Powder:** In a sulfur-scented workshop run by a gruff female dwarf, the party purchased five kegs of **Ghost Powder** (highly volatile explosives that burn with necrotic, pitch-black flames) and thirty rounds of specialized necrotic bullets. They packed the dangerous cargo into their newly acquired *bags of holding*.

---

## The Obsidian City of the Dead

Before leaving the city, Skal insisted on visiting his faction, the **Looking Glass Covenant**. He led the adventurers to an overgrown, mist-shrouded graveyard on the edge of town. 

Descending a hidden stone staircase, the environment dramatically shifted into polished obsidian. After a long walk through dark tunnels, they emerged overlooking a breathtaking, subterranean city where the living and the intelligent undead walked arm-in-arm.

* **A Message in the Flesh:** An undead, bird-like construct slammed into Skal. Unraveling a scroll from its talons, they read a message from **Morven Ashweaver**. It confirmed Codex's warning and instructed them to aid any librarians they encountered, identifying them by a crest depicting an open book crossed by a sword and a quill.
* **The Scribe’s Counsel:** Skal led the group to **Yorick**, a eccentric Shadar-kai scribe in the Reading Room. While Yorick had no knowledge of the astrophocus, he confirmed that Fort Dreadwake possessed a secret shoreline passage leading directly into the castle. However, he warned that no one who had gone looking for the passage had ever returned alive.

---

## A Pirate’s Deception at the Gun Deck

Realizing they could not defeat thirty soldiers and a fortress head-on, the party devised a cunning plan: they would trick Edward Teach’s crew into clearing out the fortress for them. 

They entered **The Gun Deck**, a high-class tavern frequented by elite pirates. Spotting **James Black** (Edward’s second-in-command) and **Garrett Ibbins**, Frezer and Bearskin approached their table carrying two heavy kegs of ale.

* **The Lie:** Frezer spun a wild, drunken tale about a massive, uninvited party currently taking place at Fort Dreadwake. 
* **The Confusion:** Though the lie became tangled in a web of drunken slip-ups—including confusing the name of the fort with "Edward Dreadleek"—the sheer volume of alcohol smoothed over the cracks. 
* **The Result:** James Black became furious at the prospect of an uninvited party on his captain's turf. Before he could investigate further, Frezer successfully challenged him to a drinking duel, knocking the pirate commander completely unconscious.

As the tavern erupted into sea shanties and drunken chaos, the adventurers quietly slipped out into the night.

---

## Set Sail for Fort Dreadwake

Returning to their hidden airship, the party woke Daryl and prepared the vessel for flight. With the coordinates of the abandoned Fey fortress in hand, and a pirate fleet potentially on the way to cause a distraction, the crew set a course for **Fort Dreadwake**. 

The battle for the astrophocus was finally at hand.
