# Recap: The Frostlight Heist and the Flight to Shadow's Haven

## A Captain's Confession at Gears & Grime

The heavy silence of the *Gears & Grime* tavern was thick enough to cut with a dagger. Frezer—operating under his slick alias, "Felipe the Mechanic"—stood at the bar, his casual inquiry about illegally acquired goods still hanging in the air. 

A heavy, gauntleted hand came down upon his shoulder. The man attached to it wore the pristine purple and silver armor of a high-ranking soldier. This was **Kaelin Vane**, Captain of the Guard. 

"Why would a group of strangers come to a place frequented by the guard looking for illegal information?" Kaelin asked, his brow furrowing in deep confusion.

"Well," Frezer replied smoothly, gesturing to the rest of the party—including Skal and Bearksin. "You're guards. You probably know more about what's stolen than anyone. You're the best people to ask."

Kaelin Vane, unable to fault the bizarre logic, sighed and recomposed himself. He introduced himself formally and listened as the party explained they were commissioned by an Archfey named Celeste to retrieve a lost artifact: the **Astrophocus**.

Kaelin’s eyes widened. He revealed that the Astrophocus had indeed been turned over to the Crown for safekeeping, but it had recently been stolen from the vaults beneath the castle. 

According to the Captain, the heist was a coordinated attack:
* **The Distraction:** A chaotic tiefling woman with coral-like horns and long, hooked chains ran through the city, laughing hysterically and setting fires to random buildings and a local church. She reportedly cried tears of black ink.
* **The Heist:** While the guard was distracted, accomplices bypassed the vault doors entirely. They left behind an unusual pool of water and carved a teleportation sigil directly onto the inside of the vault door.
* **Other Stolen Goods:** Aside from the shifting Astrophocus, the thieves broke several defensive statues and stole an enchanted bow.

Kaelin explained that the teleportation sigil on the vault door led to a clearing in the forest at the base of the mountains just outside Frostlight. Before parting ways, the group bought a round of drinks for the guards in the tavern to smooth over their rocky introduction. In the drunken revelry, Adamson—who had recently been shrunk to a smaller size—confused the Captain by showing off the literal, empty void in his chest left by his missing mana gem. 

With a lead in hand, the party slipped out of the tavern, aiming to investigate the vandalized church before heading into the frozen wilderness.

***

## Crumbling Gods and Clouded Minds

The party arrived at a massive, magically hardened temple sculpted entirely from ice. Inside, dwarven and elven laborers worked tirelessly to repair a pantheon of stone deities. Strangely, the statues were actively crumbling, shedding baseball-sized chunks of stone even as they were being patched.

While Frezer and Skal went to speak with the workers, they noticed a group of "high' elves huddled in a back corner under a dense, sweet-smelling cloud of smoke. Walking into the haze, several members of the party immediately felt the world slow down, their vision swimming. 

The stoned elves were more than happy to share their snacks and gossip:
* They claimed that a few hours prior, a group of people with "wings of light" had swept through the church, tried to steal their belongings, and vanished.
* A dwarf laborer confirmed that the statues had been crumbling for months, long before the recent Manta Ray attack.
* The tiefling arsonist had targeted a statue of **Melora**, a goddess who once wandered the material plane. The fire had completely destroyed her likeness.

Hoping to halt the decay, Frezer approached the crumbling statues. He knelt and offered a deeply sincere prayer. To the amazement of the onlookers, the stone stopped shedding, its decay slowing to a crawl. 

Moving to a stone statue of a great tree, Adamson offered another prayer, wishing for strength. As he prayed, a strange warmth bloomed in his chest. The empty void left by the mana gem began to knit together, filled by the magic of the stone tree. 

With Adamson whole once more and the high wearing off, the party resolved to leave the city behind and head for the teleportation clearing.

***

## Shimmers in the Snow

A few hours of trekking through the sub-zero temperatures brought the party to the mountain clearing. The snow was heavily trampled. There were horse hoofprints, fire pits of cold charcoal, and flattened rectangular depressions in the snow indicating a camp of about fifty small tents and one massive central pavilion. 

Bearksin, utilizing his magic to speak with a local squirrel, learned that a "strange lady with many friends" had been here "28 acorns ago" (roughly a week) before moving deeper into the mountains. 

As they searched the abandoned camp, Adamson’s sharp eyes caught a strange, localized shimmer in the air. Sensing an invisible watcher, he packed a snowball tight and hurled it into the empty space. 

The snowball exploded against nothingness. A startled voice cried out: *"Hey! That's not very nice!"*

Frezer immediately cast *Faerie Fire*, outlining a tall figure in glowing, violet light. The figure threw back his hood, revealing a shifting, shadow-woven cloak that seemed to absorb the light. This was **Thelon**, a wood elf from Frostlight.

***

## A Rogue's Gallery and the Flight to Shadow's Haven

Thelon claimed he had not been following the party, but had overheard them speaking of the Astrophocus at the tavern. He revealed that he, too, was seeking the artifact to return it to the vault of King Vane. 

Recognizing they shared a common enemy, Thelon agreed to share what he knew. He warned the party that the thieves were a dangerous, highly organized mercenary crew sporting a crest of a **dragon surrounded by a thorny briar bush**. 

Thelon rattled off the key members of this rogue's gallery:
* **The Satyr:** Wears bark armor; has a shattered, hollow chest.
* **The Changeling:** Cries black tears; wields a rapier forged of pure ice.
* **The Fairy:** Possesses wings that are perpetually dripping wet.
* **The Shadar-kai:** A grey-skinned elf whose flesh is made of translucent crystal.
* **The Goliath:** Missing an arm, replaced by a mass of thorny vines.
* **The Dampire:** Covered in dozens of blinking, twitching eyes.
* **The Tiefling:** The fire-starter with coral horns and hooked chains.
* **The Hobgoblin:** Wears a rusted crown wrapped in barbed wire (and apparently enjoys eating grapes).
* **The Aasimar:** Features rotting, featherless wings.
* **The Human:** Covered in crab-like, chitinous armor.
* **The Shifter:** Possesses shark-like teeth; fights on all fours.
* **The Astral Elf:** The apparent leader, clad in fluid armor made of water and wielding a sword of glass.

Hearing the description of the crest—the dragon and the briar bush—the party’s memories were jogged. They had seen this exact sigil once before: carved into the stone walls of the mysterious well.

Thelon suggested that a crew this notorious and on the run would only head to one place: **Shadow's Haven**. 

The party retreated to their camouflaged airship, the *Zephyr's Dart*. With Bearksin at the helm, they took to the skies, flying low and slow to avoid detection. Bearksin kept a desperate eye out for his lost Trident along the way, but the snow-covered peaks yielded nothing.

As the hours bled away, the *Zephyr's Dart* crossed the freezing waters and descended into a thick, perpetual mist. Looming out of the fog was the chaotic silhouette of Shadow's Haven. 

The city was a sprawling, damp graveyard of ships. Patchwork buildings were constructed from old hulls, masts, and tattered sails. Notably, there were no advertisements for the "Happily Ever After" corporation here. At the center of this lawless port sat a massive fortress belonging to **Edward Teach**, the powerful and reluctant de facto ruler of the haven. 

The party brought their ship down quietly into the mist. They had arrived in the den of thieves, and the hunt for the Astrophocus was about to begin.
