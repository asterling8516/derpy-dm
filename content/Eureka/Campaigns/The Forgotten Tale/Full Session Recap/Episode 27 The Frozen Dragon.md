# Session Recap: The Frozen Leviathan & The Shards of Stillwater

## Act I: Relics of the Past & Ancient Schematics

Following their desperate escape from the library archives—leaving Codex behind with a barbarian guard to face his sentence—the party found temporary sanctuary within Mother’s realm. Amidst the strangely plastic-covered furniture and quiet domesticity, the companions took time to identify their newly acquired artifacts:

*   **Bearksin** donned the **Aggravated Skeletal Armor**, a cursed demonic scale mail that weaves shadows around the wearer to bolster stealth and project retaliatory magic against attackers. He also claimed a mighty **+3 Fire Great Axe**.
*   **Odin** took hold of the **Root of the Mother Tree**, a dormant staff capable of channeling powerful restoration and mass healing magic.
*   **Frezer** placed the **Knoweth-the-All** tiara upon his brow, dramatically expanding his intellect and unlocking the ability to hold multiple magical concentrations simultaneously. Upon inspecting Nick's journal, Frezer made a startling discovery: hidden within its pages was not just a guide to godhood, but a complete schematic for constructing a **Golem’s Core**—the foundation needed to build a brand-new Codex.
*   **Skal** and the rest of the party gathered their belongings as their companion group rested, preparing for whatever lay beyond Mother's doors.

Before departing, the heroes stripped their ship of its *Happily Ever After* metal cladding, casting off their former disguise to fly under their own banner once more.

---

## Act II: Shadows Over the Ocean

Setting a course toward the northern **Stillwater Shards**, the party took to the skies with **Bearksin** at the helm. As they sailed through the clouds, the world below offered grim reminders of escalating chaos:

1.  **The Oceanic Maw:** Deep below, the ocean opened into a terrifying, tooth-lined abyss—a massive trench swallowing sea life and currents whole before snapping shut beneath the waves.
2.  **Teach’s Armada:** Scanning the horizon, **Skal** spotted dozens of floating vessels uprooted directly from Shadow’s Haven, heading inland toward Uther. Teach’s war had officially begun.
3.  **A Silent Refusal:** A cold voice echoed within Skal’s mind, demanding to know why they were not marching alongside Teach's forces. Skal chose silence, letting the entity's disappointment wash over him as they pushed further north.

---

## Act III: The Frostbound Tribe

The air grew bitterly frigid as they approached the **Stillwater Shards**. Above the central island hung a colossal, pitch-black *Happily Ever After* airship—its hull shimmering like oil, actively mining into the jagged peaks below.

Deciding to avoid a direct aerial assault, the party landed their ship on a neighboring islet, expertly concealing it beneath pine branches and heavy snowbanks. Using their trust-tested **Flying Carpet**, **Frezer**, **Adamson**, **Odin**, **Bearksin**, and **Skal** skimmed low over the freezing waters to land unseen on the main island.

```
                  [ Stillwater Shards ]
                            │
        ┌───────────────────┴───────────────────┐
        ▼                                       ▼
[ Black H.E.A. Airship ]              [ White-Kobold Camp ]
 (Mining the Peaks)                    (Led by Grox)
        │                                       │
        └───────────────────┬───────────────────┘
                            ▼
              [ Subterranean Ice Cavern ]
```

Slipping into the blizzard-choked woods, **Skal** melted into the shadows to scout ahead. He discovered a camp of white-scaled **Kobolds** huddled around a fire, anxiously discussing the black warship. 

Approaching peacefully, the party spoke with **Grox**, a silver-adorned Kobold elder. Grox revealed that the *Happily Ever After* forces had driven his people from their mountain ancestral home. According to local legend, an ancient dragon was buried deep within the peak, and the invaders were tirelessly excavating the ice to reach it. 

After **Bearksin** gifted the tribe a frozen keg of ale, Grox agreed to guide the party up the treacherous lower paths of the mountain.

---

## Act IV: Secrets in the Summit

Grox led the party past territorial owl-bear grounds to a vertical 60-foot cliff of solid ice. Rather than risk a dangerous climb, **Frezer** unfurled the Flying Carpet once more, whisking the entire group effortlessly to the top. 

At the summit lay the ruined ruins of the Kobold village, now patrolled by squads of *Happily Ever After* automatons pushing heavy carts of ice. Stealthily avoiding detection, the heroes slipped into the abandoned Chief’s Manor. Inside, amidst scattered pelts and forgotten toys, they uncovered two vital things:
*   Two **Potions of Healing**.
*   An ancient **Illustrated Scroll** detailing the realm's history: *A five-headed dragon tearing through planar portals, hundreds of dragons ascending to the sky, and a single majestic blue dragon plummeting into the sea—freezing the surrounding waters upon impact to form the Stillwater Shards.*

---

## Act V: The Frozen Leviathan

Determined to reach the dragon before the automatons, **Frezer** spent a short rest absorbing the mechanics of *Dispel Magic*. Harnessing a burst of brilliant arcana, Frezer unravelled the magical structure of the mountain’s ice, carving a continuous, spiraling staircase straight down through the floor of the Chief’s Manor while **Bearksin** concealed the entrance with a heavy pelt.

### Descent & Whispers
During the long hours of tedious tunneling:
*   Vibrations of automaton pickaxes echoed through the walls above them.
*   **Bearksin** began hearing the ghostly voice of his fiancé calling out from within his *Bag of Holding*. Suspecting a magical lure or a mind trick from the depths, Bearksin wisely kept the bag sealed.

### The Collapse
Deep beneath the daylight's reach, Frezer cast one final *Dispel Magic* on the ice cavern floor. Without warning, the icy crust gave way, sending all five adventurers plummeting into a pitch-black abyss!

Acting with lightning reflexes, **Frezer** whipped out the Flying Carpet mid-fall. The magical fabric expanded just in time, catching **Adamson**, **Odin**, **Skal**, **Bearksin**, and **Frezer** before they struck the cavern floor.

As the carpet stabilized in the soft blue glow of the subterranean expanse, the party looked down in absolute awe. Suspended inside a crystal-clear monolith of ancient, magical ice, caught mid-descent as if frozen in a single heart-beat of time, rested a colossal **Ancient Blue Dragon**.
