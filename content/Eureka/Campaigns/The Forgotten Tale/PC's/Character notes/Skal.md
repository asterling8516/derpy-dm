# [[Skal Deathchewer]] - Notes
## Backstory
[[Skal Deathchewer|Skal]] grew up in Shadow Heaven in the Kingdom of Clubs. A naturally curious rat with an insatiable appetite for arcane knowledge. Learning the ways of life and death magic from his clawed hands, while able to toss a fireball where needed. Despite his overall rough look, [[Skal Deathchewer|Skal]] is more of a scholar than a fighter.
