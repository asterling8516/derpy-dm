# [[Odin Steelwing]] - Notes
## Backstory
Growing up in **Frostlight**, you were always an outsider due to being an **Owlin**. Your unique features and nocturnal tendencies set you apart, but you had an unquenchable thirst for knowledge, admiring the city's unique blend of law and mechanics.

You admired the **Aether-Wrights** and the other blacksmiths, and often spent time watching your father work at his forge: **The Nocturnal & Elemental**. Your father specialized in clockwork machines and utility items, while you often gazed at the great, silent constructs that patrolled the streets—from the **Iron Golems** enforcing the Iron Law to the self-hauling **steam carts** that moved goods. You began trying your hand at the craft yourself, turning leftover scrap from the forge into small mechanical trinkets.

You understood the importance of the city's **Laws**, which defined your life and your father's trade:
- **Magic is only to be used to craft magic items** to ensure side effects are controlled and that **The Cataclysm** is never repeated.
- **All serve The Kingdom of Spades,** including the King and Queen, maintained by a council of representatives from each mortal plane race.
- **Those caught using magic outside of crafting MUST be reported immediately.**

As you grew older, a simple **puppet shop** opened across the street. You met the owner, an older human named **Geppetto**, who taught you the delicate craft of hand-carved wooden figures. You soon realized Geppetto wasn't just carving wood; through his shop window, you saw him making small **clockwork puppets** that moved.
