# Adamson - Notes
## Backstory
Originally starting as a **normal tree** before the great cataclysm, you have lived a long life. After the collision of planes, you found yourself planted in what would come to be known as the **The Black Forest**, which lies just outside the border of **The Kingdom of Spades**. While living there as a tree you saw a great many evils: the horrors of **The great war**, hags luring children into the forest with candy, and creatures killing humanoids in front of you. You have also seen the great powers that have come to replace the old gods, like **The Three Sisters** of the Fickle Fates (Altheria, Morwen, Calistia) and the mother of hags herself, whom you've only known as **Mother** as the other hags called her that.

One day, out of boredom, she brought you to life. It wasn't a ritual, but a simple, terrifying gesture: she placed her palm on your bark and whispered one word, and in that instant, you were fully awakened. You felt the world not just on your bark, but in a mind that could _think_ and _move_. As the other hags from the forest disbanded and fled, she took you in.

You lived with her for a while in the forest, and she taught you all she knew. She trained you in the **old ways of the druids**, showing you how to **cast spells** and **fight monsters** to protect the forest (and herself). She also taught you to read and write, using ancient texts that chronicled the truth of history from before all **The Cataclysm**'s mayhem.

Your life with her shattered one eve when you saw her finalize a contract with a desperately poor farmer. The deal saved the man's farm, but it stole his youngest daughter's ability to ever feel joy again. You finally understood: she was not just a watcher of the forest, but a **direct cause** of the pain and imbalance you had witnessed for centuries. The moral center you had developed as a passive observer could not stand the active corruption.
