The capital city of the Kingdom of Spades, Frostlight, is a marvel of both nature and technology, a perfect reflection of its people's pragmatic and logical nature. It sits nestled in a great valley within the frigid **Frosted Plains**, at the foot of a towering, snow-capped mountain range. The city itself is a living machine, with its foundations built not just on the cold ground but on the roots of colossal, enchanted trees. These trees, the "Gears of the Grove," have been infused with the magic of the Material Plane, their trunks and branches interwoven with intricate clockwork gears, polished brass pipes, and shimmering, copper-wire veins.

The city's buildings are a fascinating mix of natural and artificial materials. Towers of magically hardened ice rise alongside spires of polished steel. Quaint cottages of stone and timber stand beside workshops that hum with the quiet, rhythmic ticking of a thousand machines. The city's energy is a clean, humming magic drawn from the geysers of the mountains, which power the great clockwork gears that, in turn, sustain the city's life. The streets are clean and orderly, with no magic running rampant, and the air smells of cold iron and pine. It's a city of logic and ingenuity, a place where a fairy tale life is meticulously crafted, not wished for.
