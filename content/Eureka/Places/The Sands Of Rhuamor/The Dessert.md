A vast dessert that has formed at the location of the two intersection of the planes of the Fae Wilds and Shadowfell. This massive dessert is constantly changing and unforgiving to those who wander in unprepared water and food are often times scarce. The sands remain cool even in the heat of the day those that find themselves lost in the sands often tell stories of seeing lost loved ones or creatures that don't exist. But as they say life always finds a way. And [[Oasis City]] was born. A literal Oasis in the dessert of Rhuamor. From the wilds giant lizards were birthed and have thrived here from Shadowfell nightmarish creatures have taken refuge under the sand and lay in wait.
