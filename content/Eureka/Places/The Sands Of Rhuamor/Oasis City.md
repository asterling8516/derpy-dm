Rising from the sands are tall, flat-roofed buildings forged of sunbaked clay, stone, and brick. Their parapet walls and facades catch the desert sun, glittering with brilliant aquamarine inlays. As your eyes trace inward toward the city center, the sparse desert brush gives way to an impossibly dense, vibrant jungle of foliage.

This lush green heart climbs up the sides of a massive, stepped pyramid—a sprawling ziggurat of square homes built directly over a sparkling, life-giving aquifer.

Yet, the magic doesn't stop at the ground. Drifting lazily through the sky around the city are massive, upside-down stone pyramids draped in cascading hanging gardens and heavy agriculture. And suspended directly above the central ziggurat, a metallic airship platform hovers in the sky, projecting massive, neon holographic advertisements that cast a kaleidoscopic glow across the ancient stone below.

_“Where the shadows end, the water begins.”_ You have found the Oasis.

Oasis City is an architectural marvel. The outer rings consist of flat-roofed, square homes made of sunbaked clay and brick, decorated with vibrant aquamarine tiles. As one moves toward the center, the vegetation grows impossibly dense, culminating in the **Ziggurat of the Source**, a massive stepped pyramid built over the life-giving aquifer. Above it all, an airship platform projects holographic advertisements that cast neon light across the ancient stone, while upside-down pyramid islands float serenely, draped in hanging gardens.
