Following the Great War of the Planes 500 years ago, the weapons and armor of the fallen were left to rust in this desolate no man's land. The area has remained untouched ever since the signing of the Treaty of Evermore. 

However, local legends surround the Iron Meadows. Some travelers whisper of seeing spectral figures wandering the fields, while others claim to have witnessed ancient battles replaying as empty suits of armor clash in the mist. One thing remains certain: those who wander too far into the meadows are never seen again.
