The Fields is a vast, open plains region that has been carefully cultivated by the elves who call it home. The landscape has been transformed into sprawling fields of crops and pastures for livestock, dotted with simple, rustic houses. 

There is a profound sense of camaraderie and community among the villagers—so much so that the adults practice communal parenting, raising all the children together as one large family. The children, as a result, run amuck and cause all kinds of mischief. It's not uncommon to see one group of kids running around starting fires, only for another group to come by and pee on the flames to put them out! While they are a deeply hospitable people, always willing to lend a helping hand, they are largely pacifists and not known as fighters.

The elves of The Fields are also famous for their unique local creation known as "crickwater." Highly potent and packed with both alcohol and caffeine, crickwater is actually huffed rather than drank, providing an intense and immediate rush.

The founding of The Fields is a local legend: a pair of elves fell in love and were ostracized from their former community over accusations of being first cousins. The founders adamantly claimed they were third cousins at best, though most suspect the truth lies somewhere in the middle.
