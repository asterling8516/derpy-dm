> "Through the persistent, cold mist, the jagged silhouette of Shadow's Haven rises like a graveyard of giants. The settlement is a chaotic patchwork of architecture, entirely constructed from the splintered hulls, snapping masts, and rotting quarterdecks of traditional, wind-driven sailing ships. Tattered canvas sails act as awnings, and the skeletal remains of old pirate galleons form the walls of taverns and homes. The air smells of old brine, shadow-magic, and wet wood. The inhabitants—mostly pale, hollow-eyed refugees of the Shadowfell—move quietly through the damp streets, casting long, unnatural shadows. High above, perched in the fused remains of three massive ship sterns, sits the 'palace' of the man who reluctantly holds this driftwood empire together."


### The Reluctant Leader: Edward Teach
Edward Teach-  never asked to be a ruler. 
	"Rhumor has it Edward showed up here kick some giant monsters ass sent it packing and took over the island"

- **His Demeanor:** He is perpetually annoyed by the bureaucracy of leadership. If villagers come to him with a dispute over stolen rations, he's likely to just throw a bag of coins at them and yell at them to get out of his sight.
    
- **Why they follow him:** Despite his gruff, terrifying exterior, he is fiercely protective of his "crew" (which now unfortunately includes the whole town). He complains constantly, but if an outside threat comes for Shadow's Haven, Teach is the first one out the door with a cutlass and a loaded flintlock.
#### The Queen Anne's Revenge 
Teach's ship is feared and revered through out Shadows Haven. For some it shows protection and stands as a symbol of Teach's Power for others it holds a place fear strong enough to keep people in line. 

# Important Locations
## The Gun Deck
A local tavern that was built out of the hull of an old war ship. The tables are old empty powder kegs. Its one of only a few places that get loud. This is where you will find your common folk.

## The Crow's Nest
The highest point in Shadows Haven by far. It was made from masts of old ships and is used as a lookout for airships or other possible threats

## The Jolly Roger Apothecary
A small building made from the remains of a medical frigate. Ran by a Shadar-Kai named Dr. Silas Bone, who specializes in treating Shadows Rot. 

## The Keelhaul Market
Made up of the ribcage of an ancient galleon this market has what you need from information to magic items.

## The Brig
For the rare instances where someone is put in jail instead of being kicked out of Shadows Haven or Killed

## The Weeping Figureheads
A strange place that only a few dare venture. Stories are told that under the right conditions the figureheads come to life and begin whispering secrets of the mist

# The Four Familes
Four families of pirates, thieves, and mercenaries make up the powers that keep Shadows Haven and much of The Kingdom of Clubs functioning

## The Shadoweavers
A family of humans who specialize in information gathering and reselling. They are also very capable when it comes to acquiring things that may or may not be in the possession of others. 

## The Kidd's
Ran by William Kidd he was once one of the top brass for H.E.A. and most of their men are made up of other old members of H.E.A. who have used their knowledge of the corporation to hide Shadows Haven's treasure for teach. They have been hiding the treasure in pocket dimensions and are the people to see when seeking a safe house or a secure place to store things. While they are pirates they hold up their end of the deal when one is made to hide something or someone. These guys are professional smugglers. 

## The Bonny-Read Syndicate
These guys or should I say ladies are ran by Anne Bonny and Mary Reed. A couple of shadar-ki who have taken to warfare. They defend the kingdom of Clubs from threats mostly foreign threats either from the seas or skies. These guys are essentially shock troopers with fast ships and boarding equipment. 

## The Roberts 
A group of "enforcers" who collect taxes from shadows haven and enforce teaches laws. Though there are few. They dont interfere with most disputes, but will act as mediators for disputes and will over see acts of parlay a sanctioned dual to settle disputes between citizens though there must be grounds for the parlay like removing a bad captain or getting back stolen treasure.
