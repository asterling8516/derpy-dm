Infernium know as the city of Gilded Temptation! Where your hearts desires come to life. Many have found fame and fortune among the pleasures that Infernium brings. Though some would argue that many more have lost the very same. Constructed after the great cataclysm and before the treaty was was signed the city has taken on a mountainous look. Each of the layers of hell leading up to greater layers. The top of the mountain has gained the name [[The Spire of Power]] all manipulation and illusions fade away here where only the strongest of devils reside. The strongest being the current High Queen Asmodea. She rules her domain with level head though many try and persuade her to there favor. The city is alight at all times with neon glows from casinos and other events trying to get passerby's to come and spend coin. Like Night City from cyber punk meets Las Vegas.
