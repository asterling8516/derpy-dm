Grimhold is a breathtaking, terrifying collision of untamed Feywild magic and ruthless corporate efficiency. It is the seat of power for the Kingdom of Diamonds, but the true royalty here isn't flesh and blood—it's the bottom line of the mega-corporation **Happily Ever After (H.E.A.)**.

The city is built directly into and around a massive, ancient fey forest. Sky-scraping towers of steel and glass are woven into the trunks of trees the size of mountains. Neon holograms of corporate mascots flicker alongside swarms of real, glowing pixies.

### The Rulers: Dee and Dum

Technically, Grimhold is ruled by the Archfey siblings **Tweedle Dee** and **Tweedle Dum** (and as the locals wisely say, _never_ add the 'B').

- **The Vibe:** They are unpredictable, dangerous, and utterly bored. They dress in absurd, hyper-extravagant corporate-fey fashion (think pinstripe suits made of actual woven gold and spider-silk, with massive, ridiculous hats).
    
- **The Deal:** Centuries ago, the twins found ruling to be too tedious. When H.E.A. emerged as a power player, the twins struck a deal: H.E.A. handles all the actual laws, infrastructure, and policing, and in return, Dee and Dum get unlimited wealth, endless entertainment, and the right to invent bizarre, arbitrary laws whenever they feel like it just to watch people scramble.
