# Sir Talavar

A noble Faerie Dragon who serves as a knight of the Summer Court. 

Sir Talavar is exceptionally polite, chivalrous, and valiant, often speaking with the formal cadence of a true knight. He was sent to Prismeer to investigate rumors of Zybilna's disappearance but ended up captured by the forces of the Hourglass Coven. He is typically found trapped in a birdcage in [[Hither]], relying on friendly adventurers to help him escape.
