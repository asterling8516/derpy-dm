# Thaco

The clown who guards the staff area of the Witchlight Carnival. 

Unlike a typical cheerful clown, Thaco is a gruff, retired human adventurer who still wears his full clown makeup but acts completely un-clown-like. He is strictly business and doesn't tolerate rule-breakers.

He has a magical pipe that he smokes from; instead of smoke, it blows bubbles that sometimes pop to reveal shapes, words, or memories.
