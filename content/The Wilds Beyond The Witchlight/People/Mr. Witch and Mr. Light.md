# Mr. Witch and Mr. Light

The current proprietors of **The Witchlight Carnival**. They acquired the carnival from its previous owner, an eladrin named Isolde.

- **Mr. Witch:** A stoic, serious Shadar-kai who manages the business logistics of the carnival. He dresses impeccably and always carries the magical **Witchlight Watch**, which dictates the schedule of the carnival.
- **Mr. Light:** A flamboyant, theatrical human who serves as the face of the carnival. He is incredibly colorful and carries the **Witchlight Vane**, a magical weather vane that controls the mood and weather of the carnival based on the attendees' happiness.
