# Jingle Jangle

A friendly goblin who resides in Telemy Hill, within the domain of [[Hither]].

Jingle Jangle is obsessed with keys. She wears a massive coat completely covered in jingling keys of all shapes and sizes, which is how she gets her name. She is deeply paranoid that someone is going to steal her truffles or her keys, but she is inherently helpful to travelers if they treat her kindly (especially if they give her a new key for her collection!).
