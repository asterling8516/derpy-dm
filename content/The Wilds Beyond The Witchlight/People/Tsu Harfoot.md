# Tsu Harfoot

Tsu Harfoot is a cheerful human woman who runs the **Inn at the End of the Road**, a magical traveling inn that roams across the Domains of Delight (frequently popping up in Prismeer). 

Her inn is not a building, but rather a walking structure. Tsu is incredibly hospitable and provides a safe haven for weary travelers in the Feywild. She is known for her excellent cooking and for ensuring that the strict rules of hospitality in the Feywild are upheld within her inn.
