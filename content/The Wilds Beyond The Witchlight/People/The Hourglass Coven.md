# The Hourglass Coven

The Hourglass Coven is a trio of powerful hag sisters who have overthrown the Archfey Zybilna and fractured the domain of Prismeer into three distinct realms. They are responsible for stealing "lost things" from children who visit the Witchlight Carnival.

The sisters are:
- **Bavlorna Blightstraw (The Lorna):** A horrid hag who rules the swampy domain of **[[Hither]]**. She is obsessed with taxidermy, toad-like creatures, and creating miniature clones of herself (lurchers). She resides in a rotting stilt-house.
- **Skabatha Nightshade (Granny Nightshade):** She rules the enchanted forest of **Thither** from her domain in Loomlurch. She appears as an old woman with a wind-up key in her back and is obsessed with toys and capturing children to work in her factory.
- **Endelyn Moongrave (Creeping Lyn):** She rules the mountainous, stormy domain of **Yon** from her theater, Motherhorn. She is a master of tragedy and destiny, capable of predicting the future, and she views the world entirely as a theatrical play.
