# Kettlesteam

A mischievous Kenku (a raven-like humanoid) who is a warlock with a fey patron (specifically, Zybilna). 

She has been causing issues around the Witchlight Carnival in an attempt to figure out what happened to her patron, as she knows the carnival owners (Mr. Witch and Mr. Light) are hiding something. She communicates only by mimicking sounds she has heard, and notably, she stole the voice of the mime **Candlefoot**.
