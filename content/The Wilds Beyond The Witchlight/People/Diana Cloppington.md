# Diana Cloppington

Diana runs the Unicorn Carousel at the Witchlight Carnival. 

She appears to be a centaur, but her lower half is not that of a real horse. Instead, she has the painted, wooden body of a carousel horse. She originally made a deal with the hag Skabatha Nightshade to save her dying horse, but the hag twisted the deal, permanently fusing Diana with her horse and turning its body to painted wood.
