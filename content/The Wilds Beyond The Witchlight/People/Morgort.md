# Morgort

A brave Bullywug (frog-person) knight who belongs to the Soggy Court in the domain of [[Hither]]. 

Unlike many of her sycophantic peers in the Soggy Court, Morgort is genuinely honorable and takes her vows of knighthood very seriously. She rides a giant toad mount and is determined to uphold chivalry in a swamp that is largely corrupt and absurd.
