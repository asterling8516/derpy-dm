# Agdon Longscarf

A notorious Harengon (rabbit-folk) bandit and thief who roams the swamps of [[Hither]]. 

Agdon is impossibly fast, highly arrogant, and loves to dramatically steal things from travelers before they even realize what hit them. He wears a ridiculously long, magical bright blue scarf trailing behind him, which serves as both his namesake and his prized possession. He leads a gang of Harengon bandits and has made a pact with the hag Bavlorna Blightstraw, making him immune to the exhausting effects of the [[Hither]] swamps.
