# Dirlagraun

Dirlagraun is a majestic Displacer Beast (a panther-like creature with six legs and two tentacle-whips on its shoulders) who mans the Lost Property wagon at the Witchlight Carnival. 

Despite their fearsome appearance, Dirlagraun is quite friendly and gently cares for lost items and lost children. They are deeply saddened because their own cub, a young displacer beast named **Star**, has gone missing.
