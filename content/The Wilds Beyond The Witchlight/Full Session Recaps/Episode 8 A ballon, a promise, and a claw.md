# The Soggy Court of Downfall

## An Ill-Fated Crossing
The journey through the swampy realm of [[Hither]] took a chaotic turn at the edge of the river. The great stone bridge across the waters of Luna and Lucenya had collapsed after the adventurers—**Luna**, **Lucenia**, **Lashakira**, **Lainey**, and **La-Aaron**—unwittingly angered a massive war boulder. 

To make matters worse, **Lashakira** and **Lucenia** tumbled into the murky depths just as two menacing merfolk emerged from the water. With unnaturally wide jaws lined with shark-like teeth and elongated claws, the creatures lunged. 

* **The Ambush:** The merfolk clawed and bit viciously, dragging **Lashakira** beneath the surface. Despite her shifting into a feral beast to bite back, she was overwhelmed by their claws and fell unconscious into the dark current.
* **The Struggle:** **Lucenia** desperately tried to leap from boulder to boulder to escape the water, only to slip and splash back in. **Lainey** invoked a spell of swift movement (*Expeditious Retreat*) to scramble onto a high stone next to **Luna**.
* **The Rescue Spirit:** From the shoreline, **Luna** fired her crossbow and conjured a *Healing Spirit*—which manifested as a glittering, wand-waving fairy godmother throwing glowing dust across the dark waters. The magic revived **Lashakira** just in time, but a heavy harpoon plunked into **Luna**, dragging her off her perch and into the river.
* **The Bullywug Intervention:** Just as the party was on the verge of being completely overwhelmed, a small wooden boat filled with bullywug archers drifted down the river. They loosed a volley of arrows at the merfolk. Sensing the tide turning, the aquatic terrors retreated into the deep.

Bruised, waterlogged, and coughing up swamp water, the party dragged themselves to the far shore to take a short, much-needed rest.

***

## The Smoldering Workshop
Further up the shore, the party discovered a strange wooden structure built on high stilts. Its steep, conical thatched roof was blackened, and thick, acrid smoke curled from its top. The air smelled heavily of charred wood.

Peering inside, they found the workshop in a state of ruin. Burnt shelves and broken balloon parts littered the floor. A frantic bullywug in a leather smock was running around with a bucket, chasing two mischievous, bouncing embers—living coals that cackled as they hopped from beam to beam, igniting new fires.

* **Extinguishing the Threat:** Wanting to help, **Luna** grabbed a massive bucket of water and doused one of the coals, smashing it into harmless ash. **La-Aaron** quickly followed suit, drawing a shortsword to cleave the second cackling ember before it could cause more damage.
* **The Reward:** Grateful for their swift action, the bullywug mechanic introduced himself. He explained that rebel bullywugs had broken in and set the fires. In gratitude, he gifted the party a set of magical insectoid brooches with wings that gently fluttered:
  * **Luna** received a **Bronze Fly Brooche**.
  * The rest of the party received **Steel Fly Brooches**.
  * *Note:* The mechanic warned them that these brooches serve as symbols of the "Soggy Court" and must be worn if they wish to speak with King Gollum XIX or the witch, Bavlorna.

***

## The Bridge of Severed Kings
To reach the king, the party had to cross a flat wooden plank bridge spanning an arm of the lake. The path was lined with a gruesome sight: wooden pikes bearing the severed, yet still-living, heads of sixteen former bullywug monarchs.

Sitting at the center of the bridge, dangling his feet over the water, was a small, three-foot-tall scarecrow child with pincer hands and a hollow gourd for a head. He was talking to the severed heads, who erupted into a cacophony of bickering voices as the party approached.

* **The Lore of the Heads:** The heads explained that they were Kings Gollum I through XVI. In bullywug tradition, each king had murdered his predecessor to take the throne, only to be murdered in turn. 
* **Clapper Claw:** The scarecrow child introduced himself as **Clapper Claw**. He revealed he was searching for his stolen head—a stag skull—which had been taken by the cover-dwelling witch, **Bavlorna**, simply because she thought it would be funny. 
* **A New Companion:** Feeling pity for the child, **Luna** offered to help retrieve his head. **Clapper Claw** agreed to guide them through the swamp, tucking his belongings into a pouch of moldy straw and joining their ranks.

***

## Audience with King Gollum XIX
The party crossed the bridge to a grand, askew marble gazebo resting on a mound of soggy earth. Lounging on muddy pillows were lazy bullywug nobles, listening to a harpist while gossiping in low, wet croaks. 

On an ornate throne sat **King Gollum XIX**, a flabby bullywug wearing a crown of woven lily flowers, petting a small crocodile on his lap.

* **The King's Quest:** King Gollum XIX noticed their fly brooches and acknowledged them as temporary members of his Soggy Court. He offered them a deal: if they delivered a massive, leather-bound tome called the ***Big Book of Bad Blood*** to **Bavlorna**, they would be allowed to keep their brooches and speak with the witch.
* **The Book:** **Luna** flipped through the book and found it was a list of names and grievances, ranging from minor slights to outright rebellions. The party accepted the book, planning to use the errand as their ticket into Bavlorna's presence.

***

## Exploring the Bayous of Downfall

With the King's book in hand, the party set out to explore the surrounding islands of Downfall before confronting the witch.

### 1. The Jailhouse Rebel (Area D8)
They found a wooden hut on stilts containing mangrove-root prison cells. Slumped in the stinking water of one cell was **Morgort**, a bullywug knight in rags.
* **The Connection:** Morgort revealed she had been imprisoned for helping her friend, **Wigglewock**, escape with **Sir Talibar** (the fae dragon the party had saved earlier). She was saddened to hear rumors of Wigglewock's demise but heartened to know Talibar was free.
* **The Escape Plan:** Morgort told them that if they could steal the key from King Gollum's guards and free her, she could pilot a nearby hot air balloon to take them to the realm of **Yon**.

### 2. The Trial by Combat Grounds (Area D9)
They passed a heavily churned mud pit surrounding a mangrove tree draped in heavy iron chains and shackles. Scattered, rusting armor and dried blood pools suggested this was Downfall’s brutal gladiator arena.

### 3. The Hall of Croaks (Area D11)
A grand hall built into the roots of a massive tree, filled with gossiping bullywug courtiers. The party boasted of their exploits, earning some thrown goblets of wine and learning about a mysterious merchant balloon nearby.

***

## The Raincloud Bazaar
Floating over the lake, moored by heavy ropes, was a large, dark raincloud contained within a net. Hanging beneath it was a black wicker basket serving as a shop called *Trinkets, Baubles, and Charms*. 

The shop was run by two dark-skinned elves with solid black eyes: **Trinket** (male) and **Bauble** (female). They did not deal in gold, but rather in conceptual, whimsical fey bargains:

| Item | Merchant's Price |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Moonlight Monocle** (Goggles of Night) | The color of your eyes *or* the rhythm of your step |
| **Bundle of Dry Wood** | The rhythm of your step |
| **Dust Mallow Pie** | A secret |

* **Luna's Bargain:** Desiring the **Moonlight Monocle**, **Luna** agreed to trade the color of her eyes to **Trinket**. The elf produced two colorless glass marbles, and as he rolled them in his hands, the vibrant color drained from Luna's irises, leaving her eyes entirely void-black. Though her eyes looked empty to others, her vision remained perfectly intact, and she claimed the monocle.
* **A Ride to Yon:** The siblings offered to fly the party to Yon in their raincloud balloon, but their price was steep: *two eyes and two steps* (the color of two people's eyes and the rhythm of two people's steps).

***

## The Flight of Clapper Claw
The bargain took a dark turn when the party jokingly asked **Clapper Claw** if he would give up his "steps" (his ability to dance and walk) to pay for the balloon ride. 

Terrified of having yet another part of his body stolen, the scarecrow child panicked and fled into the misty swamp, crying out that they were no better than the witch who stole his head.

* **The Search:** The party ran back to the bridge of severed heads. The bickering bullywug heads mocked them, shouting that they had scared the boy away.
* **The Misty Clothesline:** Following a clue from the workshop, they traced a long clothesline stretching from the burnt factory out over the foggy lake. 
* **The Rescue:** Taking a borrowed bullywug boat, they rowed into the mist and found **Clapper Claw** dangling petrified from the clothesline over the dark water. 
* **A Promise Kept:** **Luna** called out to him, offering a sincere apology and making a binding promise: *she would never steal from him, and she would protect him.* Assured by her words, the scarecrow dropped safely into their boat.

With their guide restored, their boat floating in the misty waters, and the *Big Book of Bad Blood* tucked away, the adventurers prepared to row deeper into the swamp to face whatever lay ahead.
