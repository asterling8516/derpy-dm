# Hither

Hither is the first of the three splintered realms of **Prismeer** (the other two being Thither and Yon). It is a Domain of Delight in the Feywild that has fallen under the oppressive rule of the hag **Bavlorna Blightstraw**.

Once a beautiful, vibrant wetland under the rule of the Archfey Zybilna, Hither has been corrupted into a vast, gloomy, waterlogged swamp. The air smells of decay, the skies are perpetually overcast with a strange, stagnant haze, and the terrain is dotted with overgrown fungi, sinking mud, and strange fey creatures. 

Navigation through Hither is notoriously difficult without a guide or magical means (like a swamp gas balloon) due to the shifting muck and treacherous waters.
