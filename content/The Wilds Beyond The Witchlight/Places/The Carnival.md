# The Witchlight Carnival

A place of magic, wonder, strange games, whimsical rides, and amazing prizes. Here you can find people from all walks of life and from across the many planes. The carnival is run by **Mr. Witch and Mr. Light** and a small army of Witchlighters. 

The Carnival visits a world once every eight years. It is famous for its strict adherence to three rules: The Rule of Hospitality, The Rule of Ownership, and The Rule of Reciprocity. 

Notable locations within the Carnival include:
- **The Ticket Booth:** Where visitors pay for entry (sometimes with things other than money).
- **The Carousel:** A magical ride run by Diana Cloppington.
- **Silversong Lake:** A peaceful body of water where the mermaid Palasha sings.
- **The Hall of Illusions:** A confusing maze of mirrors where the mime Candlefoot works.
- **The Lost Property Wagon:** Run by the displacer beast Dirlagraun.
- **The Big Top:** Where the main events and Mr. Light's extravaganzas take place.
