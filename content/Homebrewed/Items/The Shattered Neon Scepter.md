# Item Type
Weapon (Mace or Club)

#### Rarity
Rare, Requires Attunement

### Description
You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. When you hit with it, the target takes an extra `2d6` elemental damage (your choice).

**Glitch-Burst:** The scepter has 3 charges (regains `1d3` at dawn). As an action, you can expend 1 charge to strike the ground, releasing a blinding, holographic shockwave. Each creature within 15 feet of you must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or be **Blinded** until the end of your next turn.
