(Uncommon, Requires Attunement)**
    
    - _Wondrous Item (Headpiece)_
        
    - A jagged, uncomfortable crown made of obsidian.
        
    - While wearing this crown, you have advantage on saving throws against Illusions.
        
    - **Razor Retribution:** When a creature hits you with a melee attack, you can use your reaction to shatter a piece of the crown outward, dealing `1d6` slashing damage to the attacker.
