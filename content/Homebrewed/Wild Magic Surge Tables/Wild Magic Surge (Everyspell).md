| 49-51 | Your clothes instantly become soaking wet (but you take no damage). | Instant |
| 52-54 | You are completely and randomly dazzled by your own brilliance. You have Disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of your next turn. | Until the end of your next turn |

| 58-60 | You swap places with a random willing creature within 30 ft. If no willing creature, you fall Prone. | Instant |
| 61-63 | You instantly and painlessly shed all body hair (including eyebrows). | Permanent (until growth/another surge) |

| 67-69 | You gain a minor magical item (e.g., a spoon that changes temperature, a glove that always smells like peppermint). | Permanent (until lost/consumed) |

| 73-75 | The next time you speak, you accidentally replace one word with the name of a type of cheese. | Instant |

| 82-84 | You emit a faint, calming aura. All creatures within 10 ft (including foes) are less interested in combat and have disadvantage on their next attack roll against you. | Instant |

| 97-98 | Bad Surge: You are now Frightened of a random, inanimate object within 30 ft of you. | 1d4 minutes |
| 99-100 | Good Surge: You instantly regain a single use of a spell slot one level lower than the one you just cast (minimum 1st level). | Instant |
